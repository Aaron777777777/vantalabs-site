Quick questions

What does the shift pay calculator estimate?

It uses the shift times and pay information you provide to form a personal estimate of what those planned shifts may earn.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does shift pay calculator manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
