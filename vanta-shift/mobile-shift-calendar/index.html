Quick questions

Can I keep my personal shift calendar on my phone?

Yes. Vanta Shift keeps the shifts you enter in a mobile personal calendar so upcoming work and days off are easy to check.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does mobile shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
