How a safe-zone alert is produced

Guardian safe zones use a configured area around familiar places such as Home and School. When the connected child phone reports location information that indicates it has entered or left that area, Guardian can provide an arrival or leaving update to trusted adults. This is designed for routine moments such as reaching school or leaving home, rather than following every movement throughout a journey.

A safe zone is an area rather than one exact GPS point, and the phone still needs to provide usable location information. Buildings, weak signal, permissions, battery conditions, connectivity and operating-system restrictions can affect when an update is detected or delivered. Safe-zone alerts are therefore useful family context rather than guaranteed proof of an exact arrival or departure time.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What causes a Guardian safe-zone alert?

An alert can be produced when the connected child phone reports location information indicating that it has entered or left a configured Home or School area.

Is a safe zone one exact GPS point?

No. It represents an area around a familiar place rather than one perfectly precise coordinate.

Can a safe-zone alert be delayed?

Yes. GPS, connectivity, buildings, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect location reporting and notification timing.
