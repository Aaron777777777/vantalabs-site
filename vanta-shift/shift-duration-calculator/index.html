Quick questions

How can I check the duration of a shift?

Use the start and finish times you enter to understand the length of the scheduled shift, including work that continues past midnight.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. Start and finish times can cross midnight so overnight work remains clear in the calendar.

Does shift duration calculator manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
