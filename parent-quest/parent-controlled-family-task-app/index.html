Parent-Controlled Family Task App

Give children a clear task routine while keeping the family setup, approvals and rewards under parent control.

Google Play App Store

Let children participate without running the system

Children should be able to understand their own tasks without needing access to every family setting. A simple child view keeps the focus on what needs doing next.

The parent manages profiles, assigns tasks and reviews completed work behind the Parent PIN. This creates a practical separation between taking part and being in charge.

Responsibility remains with the adult. Parent Quest supports the family routine but does not transfer account control to children.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
