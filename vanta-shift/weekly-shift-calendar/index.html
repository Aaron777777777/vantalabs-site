Quick questions

Can I review my shifts week by week?

Yes. A weekly calendar makes the shifts and rest days you have entered easier to review together for the week ahead.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. Start and finish times can cross midnight so overnight work remains clear in the calendar.

Does weekly shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
