Parent-Controlled Family Task App

Give children a clear task routine while keeping the family setup, approvals and rewards under parent control.

Google Play App Store

Let children participate without running the system

Children should be able to understand their own tasks without needing access to every family setting. A simple child view keeps the focus on what needs doing next.

The parent manages profiles, assigns tasks and reviews completed work behind the Parent PIN. This creates a practical separation between taking part and being in charge.

Children can therefore participate without becoming administrators of the family routine. They can focus on their own tasks and submitted progress while the parent retains the controls that affect assignments, approval and rewards.

Responsibility remains with the adult. Parent Quest supports the family routine but does not transfer account control to children.

Quick questions

Does each child need a personal phone for a parent-controlled task setup?

No. The task system is intended to be managed from the parent-owned device, with children using their profiles there.

Does giving a child a profile create a separate account?

No. The profile remains part of the parent-managed family setup and does not require an independent email login.

Which parts of the family task app stay under parent control?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, task assignment, approval and reward decisions.
