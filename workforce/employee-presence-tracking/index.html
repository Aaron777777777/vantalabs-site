Workplace presence

Employee Presence Tracking

See which employees are currently recorded as working, their workplace and recent attendance activity from the authorised manager view.

Google Play App Store

Keep the worker action simple and the record clear

For most businesses, Employee Presence Tracking is less about collecting another data point and more about creating a record that can be understood later. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should complete the expected attendance action so their current status can update.

The management side is about being able to see who is presently recorded as working and where follow-up may be needed. That judgement is supported by current booking status, workplace and recent attendance activity, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Workplace presence still needs sensible review. Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee presence?

The available record can include current booking status, workplace and recent attendance activity, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for employee presence?

Current status can be delayed by connectivity, permissions or the condition of the worker’s device.
