Quick questions

What should parents compare in a GPS tracker app for kids?

Look at privacy, who can access the family, place-based alerts, device status and how clearly the app explains phone-location accuracy and update limits.

Should the best family GPS app promise perfectly live tracking?

No. Phone location depends on GPS, connectivity, permissions, background activity and battery conditions, so continuous perfect tracking is not a realistic promise.

Why can Home and School alerts matter as much as the map?

They can provide useful routine milestones without requiring a parent to keep checking the child phone’s map position throughout every journey.
