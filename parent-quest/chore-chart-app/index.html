A chore chart that is easy to keep using

Paper charts are great until they disappear under a pile of school letters or nobody remembers to update them. Parent Quest keeps the current chores together on the device you already use.

You decide what goes on the list and who is responsible for it. Children can see their own jobs clearly, while approval and rewards stay under your control.

Because the chart is digital, parents can change it without starting again from scratch. A chore can be reassigned, removed or replaced as family routines change, while each child continues to see a straightforward list of the jobs that currently belong to them.

You choose what is suitable. Every child is different, so the parent or guardian decides which chores make sense for their age and routine.

Quick questions

Can parents customise the chores in the chart?

Yes. Parents decide which chores to add, which child they belong to and when the list should change as the household routine develops.

Can parents review chores after a child marks them complete?

Yes. Completion can be reviewed by the parent before progress, reward coins or rewards are confirmed.

Does the chore chart app take over the parent’s role?

No. Parent Quest organises the chart, while the parent or guardian remains responsible for the child, the chores, approval and rewards.
