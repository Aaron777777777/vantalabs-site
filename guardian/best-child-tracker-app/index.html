Families comparing location apps usually need more than a long feature list. Privacy, clear access controls, useful place alerts, device context and honest accuracy limits all matter when deciding which service fits everyday family life.

Guardian is designed for families who want a private Family Map, Home and School alerts, recent location context and SOS tools without public profiles or a social feed. The right choice depends on the routines and boundaries each family is comfortable with.
