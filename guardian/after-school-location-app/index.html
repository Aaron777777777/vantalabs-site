After School Location App, built around useful family context

The period after School is often when parents want the most useful context with the least amount of monitoring. A School departure alert can show that the journey has begun, while a Home safe-zone update can show when the connected child phone reaches home. The Family Map is there when a closer look is genuinely useful.

Guardian does not promise uninterrupted tracking between those milestones. Phone location can change with signal, buildings, permissions, battery saving and operating-system restrictions. The latest location, battery status and last-update time should be read together rather than treated as minute-by-minute surveillance.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What is after school location app used for?

Use Guardian for useful after-school location context, School departure alerts and Home arrival updates.

Can phone settings affect Guardian location updates?

Yes. GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions can affect location reporting and notification timing.

Is Guardian an emergency service?

No. Guardian provides family location and alert context. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
