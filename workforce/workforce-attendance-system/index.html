Workforce Attendance System

Record workforce attendance from mobile booking activity and give authorised managers a clearer view of working periods and workplace records.

Google Play App Store

Make the process easier to follow and review

Workforce Attendance System works best when the organisation starts with the real daily routine and uses the app to make that routine easier to record. For the worker, the expected action is to book on when work begins and book off when the working period ends. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to see whether the record is complete, plausible and ready for review, using booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history alongside the organisation’s attendance rules and correction process. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations. The practical test for Workforce Attendance System is whether someone unfamiliar with the original event can still understand the record later.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce attendance system?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for workforce attendance system?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
