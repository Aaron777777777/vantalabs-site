A clearer view of the working week

Employee Shift Calendar brings assigned work shifts, upcoming working days and the employee’s personal schedule into one place so the next working day is easier to see. Vanta Shift helps the employee see what shift comes next without relying on screenshots, messages or a paper rota. The app remains focused on assigned shifts, reminders, scheduled hours and estimated earnings.

An employee shift calendar is centred on one person’s assigned work rather than the schedules of a wider team. The app is most useful when the displayed pattern, assigned shifts and pay settings reflect the employee’s real arrangements.

Shift calendar remains a personal planning view. The calendar represents scheduled work and does not prove that a shift was completed.
