Quick questions

Can I create my own personal shift pattern?

Yes. You can build the day, night and off sequence that matches your own rota and use it as the basis of your personal calendar.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does personal shift pattern manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
