Guardian: Family Locator additional privacy notice

Last updated: 30 June 2026

Guardian is a private family location and safety app. Because Guardian may involve child profiles, family groups, location sharing, battery status and SOS alerts, this section explains additional privacy points specific to Guardian.

Information Guardian may process

Guardian may process account identifiers, family group details, child profile names entered by the parent or guardian, device connection status, setup codes, location coordinates, location accuracy, battery level, SOS alert status, timestamps and related app state needed to operate the family locator service.

Location data

Guardian uses location data to show the latest known location of connected family devices. Location updates may be used while the app is open and, where enabled and permitted by the device operating system, in the background.

Background location can be affected by device settings, permissions, battery optimisation, network availability, operating system restrictions and whether the app is running. Location information may be delayed, unavailable, inaccurate or incomplete. Guardian should not be relied on as a guaranteed real-time tracking system.

SOS alerts

Guardian may allow a connected child device to send an SOS alert to the private family group. SOS alerts are intended to notify family members inside the app and, where supported, through notifications.

Guardian is not an emergency service and does not contact police, ambulance, fire, rescue, schools, carers or any public authority. In an emergency, users must contact the appropriate emergency services directly.

Family responsibility and consent

The person setting up a family group is responsible for ensuring they have the legal right, parental responsibility, consent or other lawful basis to add family members, create child profiles, connect devices and view location information.

Guardian must not be used for stalking, covert tracking, harassment, unlawful monitoring or any other misuse.

Third-party services

Guardian may use third-party services including Firebase, Google services, Apple services, Google Maps, Apple Maps and app store billing systems. These services may process technical data needed for authentication, storage, app distribution, maps, directions, subscriptions or app functionality.

Data sharing

Guardian family data is intended to be visible only to members of the relevant private family group inside the app. Vanta Labs does not sell Guardian family location data.

Retention and deletion

Guardian app data may remain stored while a family group, account, child profile, alert or location record exists in the service. Users may request deletion of Guardian app data by contacting Vanta Labs.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us by email.
