A practical view of changing shifts

The value of weekly earnings tracker comes from turning estimated earnings calculated from available shift and hourly rate information into a clearer personal calendar. The employee can use Vanta Shift to form a clearer personal estimate of what scheduled shifts may earn. Its purpose stays limited to the employee’s own assigned schedule and personal totals.

A weekly earnings view groups estimated shift value inside one seven-day period, helping the employee compare one working week with another. The app is most useful when the displayed pattern, assigned shifts and pay settings reflect the employee’s real arrangements.

Estimated pay remains a personal planning view. Actual pay can differ because of payroll rules, deductions, premiums, overtime and employer-held information.
