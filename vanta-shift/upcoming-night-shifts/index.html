Quick questions

How can I quickly see my upcoming night shifts?

Keep future night shifts in your personal Vanta Shift calendar so upcoming working dates, times and rest days are available together at a glance.

Are calculated totals official records?

No. Hours and earnings shown by the app are personal planning figures based on the information entered.

Does upcoming night shifts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
