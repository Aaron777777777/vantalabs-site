Chore App on a Parent Phone

Run the family chore routine from your own phone, with tasks, approvals and rewards kept together in one place.

Google Play App Store

Manage the routine from the phone you already use

A family should not need a separate device for every child just to organise a few household jobs. Parent Quest is built around a parent-owned phone that can be shared when a child needs to view their list.

You keep control of the profiles, chores, approvals, reward coins and custom rewards. Children can take part in the routine without needing their own account or login.

That keeps the setup practical for families who do not want another device or login to manage. A child can use the parent’s phone to check their profile or mark progress, then hand it back while the adult keeps control of changes, approval and rewards.

The parent owns and manages the setup. Protected settings and approvals remain behind the Parent PIN on the parent’s device.

Quick questions

Does a child need their own phone to use the chore routine?

No. Parent Quest is built around the parent’s device, which can be shared briefly when a child needs to view their chores or record progress.

Does each child need an email address or separate login?

No. Child profiles stay inside the family setup and do not require independent email-based accounts.

Who remains in control when the parent phone is shared?

The parent or guardian keeps control of protected settings, task approval, reward coins and custom rewards.
