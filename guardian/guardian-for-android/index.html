Guardian Android

Family GPS tracking for Android with Guardian

Guardian is available for Android through Google Play and can be used as part of the same private Guardian family that supports connected phones across the service.

Google PlayApp Store

What Guardian does on Android

Guardian helps a private family check the latest location reported by a connected child's Android phone. Trusted adults can use the Family Map, see available battery and last-update information, and receive Home and School arrival or leaving alerts. A child can also send an SOS alert to the family. Guardian is a phone-based service, not a separate GPS watch.

Getting an Android phone ready

  1. Install Guardian: Use the Google Play button above on the phone or phones you intend to connect.
  2. Connect the child device: Complete the family and child-device setup shown in Guardian, using the phone the child will actually carry.
  3. Review location permissions: On the child phone, check Android's Settings > Apps > Guardian > Permissions. Available options vary by device; allow the location access needed for background reporting when offered.
  4. Review battery and notifications: Check whether Android's battery-saving or background restrictions are limiting Guardian, and allow relevant notifications. Menu names vary between phone manufacturers.
  5. Test together: Check the last-update time and try a Home or School alert before relying on it for your routine.

Why an Android location may be delayed

A last-reported position is not necessarily a live position. Weak connectivity, disabled location services, background restrictions, low battery and GPS conditions can delay updates. Start with our location troubleshooting checklist if a location looks old.

Quick questions

Where can families install Guardian for Android?

Guardian is available for Android through Google Play.

Why can Android battery settings matter for Guardian?

Battery management and background restrictions can affect how freely the connected phone is able to provide location updates.

Does an Android Guardian position always represent the phone’s exact current location?

No. GPS, connectivity, permissions, background restrictions and battery conditions can affect both accuracy and update timing.
