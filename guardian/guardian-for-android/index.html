Guardian Android

Guardian family location and safety tools on Android

Guardian is available for Android through Google Play and can be used as part of the same private Guardian family that supports connected phones across the service.

Google PlayApp Store

Guardian across the phones your family uses

A connected Android phone can provide available location and device context for trusted family adults, including the information used for familiar Home and School alerts and map checks when more detail is useful.

Android background behaviour and battery management can vary between phones, so location permissions, background access, connectivity and battery restrictions all matter when Guardian is expected to keep receiving updates.

An older Android map position may reflect the state of the phone rather than the person’s journey. Guardian cannot guarantee continuous or exact phone location and should be used alongside normal family communication; it is not an emergency service.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Where can families install Guardian for Android?

Guardian is available for Android through Google Play.

Why can Android battery settings matter for Guardian?

Battery management and background restrictions can affect how freely the connected phone is able to provide location updates.

Does an Android Guardian position always represent the phone’s exact current location?

No. GPS, connectivity, permissions, background restrictions and battery conditions can affect both accuracy and update timing.
