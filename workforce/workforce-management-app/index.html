Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

The question behind workforce management is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? The worker-facing process centres on seeing only the actions and information relevant to their role. It should remain understandable during busy arrivals, departures, site moves and scheduled checks.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess whether sites, access and worker records match the real organisation, using workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system and the surrounding workplace context. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review unclear ownership of setup, corrections or ongoing administration proportionately.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.
