Quick questions

Does a child-left-Home alert tell me where the child is going?

No. It indicates that the connected phone has left the Home safe zone but does not identify or guarantee the next destination.

Do I need to follow the child phone continuously after it leaves Home?

No. Guardian can use later place alerts for familiar milestones, while the Family Map is available when additional context is genuinely needed.

Can background phone restrictions affect the departure notification?

Yes. Location permissions, battery optimisation, connectivity and operating-system restrictions can affect background reporting.
