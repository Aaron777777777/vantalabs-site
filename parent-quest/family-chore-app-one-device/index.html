Quick questions

Can several children use Parent Quest without several phones?

Yes. Multiple child profiles can be managed from one parent-owned device, with each child’s chores and progress kept separate.

Do those child profiles need separate email accounts?

No. They remain profiles inside the parent-managed family setup rather than independent accounts.

Who controls the family setup when everyone uses one device?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approvals and rewards while children use the simpler profile view.
