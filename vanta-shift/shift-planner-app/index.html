Keep your shifts clear without turning it into a work system

For employees working changing patterns, shift planner app can make the week ahead much easier to read. Vanta Shift helps the employee see what shift comes next without relying on screenshots, messages or a paper rota. The app remains focused on assigned shifts, reminders, scheduled hours and estimated earnings.

The app is most useful when the displayed pattern, assigned shifts and pay settings reflect the employee’s real arrangements.

Quick questions

What does shift planner app show?

It focuses on assigned work shifts, upcoming working days and the employee’s personal schedule, using the shift information available in Vanta Shift.

What is Vanta Shift intended to help with?

Vanta Shift helps employees view assigned shifts, receive notifications and review scheduled hours and estimated pay.

Should I still check the official rota?

The calendar represents scheduled work and does not prove that a shift was completed.
