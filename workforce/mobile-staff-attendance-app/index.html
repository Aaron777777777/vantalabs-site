What the organisation actually needs to see

Mobile staff attendance is most useful when a business wants to replace scattered messages, paper records or end-of-month guesswork with one repeatable process. Workers mainly need a clear booking action at the beginning and end of attendance. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

Management value comes from being able to understand whether the attendance record is complete and needs correction. The system organises booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods; the manager still decides whether follow-up or correction is appropriate. A sensible rollout is to define the expected action, show workers how it works and agree who reviews exceptions. This matters because forgotten bookings, delayed records or unusual attendance durations can still occur during normal use.

What to keep in mind Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile staff attendance?

The relevant view can include booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.
