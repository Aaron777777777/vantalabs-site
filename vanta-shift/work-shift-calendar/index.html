Work Shift Calendar is intended to make one part of a rotating schedule easier to understand. It helps you see working days, rest days and changing times together.

Quick questions

How can a work shift calendar help with changing rotas?

Keeping your working dates, times and rest days together makes it easier to follow the personal rota you have entered and update individual shifts when needed.

Does this require a Vanta Shift account?

No. Vanta Shift is designed as a personal planner without an account or workplace setup.

Does work shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
