Your own rota, hours and pay in one place

The practical purpose of personal shift planner app is to make assigned work shifts, upcoming working days and the employee’s personal schedule easier to understand during an ordinary working week. The employee can use Vanta Shift to see what shift comes next without relying on screenshots, messages or a paper rota. Its purpose stays limited to the employee’s own assigned schedule and personal totals.

Employees should treat the app as a personal view of the information available rather than the source of workplace policy.

Shift calendar remains a personal planning view. The calendar represents scheduled work and does not prove that a shift was completed.
