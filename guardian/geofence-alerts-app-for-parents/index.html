geofence alerts for parents

Geofence alerts that fit real family routines

Guardian geofence alerts give parents a simple update when the connected child phone appears to enter or leave a configured place such as Home or School.

Google PlayApp Store

What parents receive when a safe-zone event occurs

The notification tells the trusted adult which connected child device triggered the place event and whether the phone crossed into or out of that safe-zone area.

For an ordinary school run or journey Home, the alert may be all the context a parent needs. The Family Map can still be opened when plans change, an expected update is late or more detail is useful.

Place alerts rely on the phone’s available location reports, so GPS, signal, permissions and battery restrictions can affect timing. Guardian is not an emergency service and does not guarantee immediate notification delivery.

Quick questions

What information does a Guardian geofence alert give a parent?

It identifies the connected child device and whether its phone has entered or left the configured place boundary.

Can a parent-facing geofence alert arrive later than the actual movement?

Yes. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can delay background location reporting and therefore the alert.

Should a geofence notification be relied on during an emergency?

No. Guardian is not an emergency service. Immediate danger should be handled through direct contact and the emergency services.
