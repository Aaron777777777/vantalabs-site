Check the latest phone location when plans change

When a pickup is late, a journey takes longer than expected or plans change, Guardian lets a trusted adult open the Family Map and check the connected child phone’s latest available location. Battery and last-update information provide useful context alongside the map, helping a parent understand whether the phone appears to be reporting normally rather than seeing a location point with no supporting information.

That makes Guardian useful for occasional checks rather than continuous watching. For predictable routines, Home and School alerts can provide arrival or leaving updates without repeatedly opening the map. A displayed position should still be treated as the phone’s latest available location rather than guaranteed proof of the child’s exact position. GPS, connectivity, buildings, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect both accuracy and how recently the phone has reported.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What does Guardian show when I check my child’s location?

The Family Map can show the connected child phone’s latest available location together with useful battery and last-update context.

Is the Guardian Family Map a continuous live feed?

No. Guardian shows available phone-location updates, and reporting can be affected by connectivity, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions.

What should I check if the location appears old?

Check the battery and last-update context and remember that GPS, signal, permissions and background restrictions can affect how recently the phone reports.
