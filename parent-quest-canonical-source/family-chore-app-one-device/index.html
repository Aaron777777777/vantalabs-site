One device can still support separate routines

Using one phone does not mean every child has to share the same list. Individual profiles keep responsibilities clear while avoiding the hassle of multiple family logins.

The parent can move between children, assign different chores and review progress from the same place. Each child still sees the tasks and rewards intended for them.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
