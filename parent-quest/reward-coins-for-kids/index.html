Reward Coins for Kids

Give children an easy way to see that their approved chores and tasks are moving them closer to a chosen reward.

Google Play App Store

Turn effort into progress they can follow

Longer goals can feel distant when children cannot see anything changing. A visible coin total makes each approved step easier to understand without promising a reward after every individual chore.

The parent decides which tasks contribute, how many coins they are worth and what rewards are available. Children see the progress, while the important settings remain parent controlled.

The visible total can help make a longer goal easier to understand without giving the coins a monetary meaning. Parents remain responsible for the rewards they create and for deciding how approved progress works in their own household.

The numbers are set by your family. Parent Quest does not assign fixed coin values or decide what a child should earn.

Quick questions

Do reward coins for kids represent cash?

No. Reward coins have no cash value and simply make approved in-app progress easier for a child to see.

Who decides how reward coins work for each child?

The parent decides which tasks contribute, the coin amounts used and which family rewards are available.

Can children purchase or cash out reward coins with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins are not purchasable from Vanta Labs, cannot be withdrawn for cash and are not exchangeable with Vanta Labs.
