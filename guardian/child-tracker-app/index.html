Quick questions

Can Guardian reduce the need to watch a child phone on the map?

Yes. Home and School alerts can provide familiar journey milestones while the Family Map remains available when more context is needed.

What can affect the accuracy of child tracking?

GPS conditions, buildings, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can all affect the connected phone’s reported location and timing.

Does Guardian guarantee a child’s safety?

No. Guardian provides family location and alert tools but cannot guarantee safety and is not an emergency service.
