Mobile Worker Clock-In App

Let mobile and field workers record attendance from their phones while managers review off-site bookings, workplace details and working hours.

Google Play App Store

Keep the worker action simple and the record clear

For most businesses, Mobile Worker Clock-In App is less about collecting another data point and more about creating a record that can be understood later. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should follow the same booking process while working across sites or field locations.

The management side is about being able to review distributed attendance without collecting separate messages from each site. That judgement is supported by off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Mobile and field workforces still needs sensible review. Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile worker clock-in?

The available record can include off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for mobile worker clock-in?

The organisation still needs a fallback process for weak signal, device problems and unclear site information.
