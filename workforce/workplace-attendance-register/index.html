A practical process for everyday operations

A useful workplace attendance register process begins with the real workplace routine, not with the software screen. Workers mainly need knowing that the expected booking action has been received. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

Management value comes from being able to understand who is currently recorded as working at the relevant workplace. The system organises the latest available worker status connected to attendance history; the manager still decides whether follow-up or correction is appropriate. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents confusing delayed device information with a definite absence from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

Workplace presence still needs sensible review. Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.
