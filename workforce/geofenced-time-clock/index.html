Geofenced Time Clock

Support location-aware attendance by recording booking activity with available workplace and device location context for manager review.

Google Play App Store

Replace scattered updates with one consistent record

Geofenced Time Clock is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking.

The management side is about being able to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace. That judgement is supported by booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for geofenced time clock?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for geofenced time clock?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
