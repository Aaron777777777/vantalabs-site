A focused starting point for family location

Guardian’s core experience is designed to keep family location straightforward. A parent can connect a child profile and use private family-location tools such as the Family Map and useful Home and School place alerts, with battery and update context available when more detail is needed. The product stays focused on family location and safety rather than adding public profiles or unrelated social features.

Guardian Premium is intended for families who need support for additional child profiles. This page does not change or imply different pricing or unsupported feature limits beyond that distinction. Guardian is available on Android through Google Play and on iPhone through the App Store, and phone-location accuracy still depends on GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system behaviour.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
