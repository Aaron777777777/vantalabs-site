What the organisation should expect from the process

Businesses normally consider employee hours tracking because the current process is slow to check, difficult to consolidate or too dependent on memory. A clear process tells the worker to review recorded hours and flag anything that appears incomplete, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to check the underlying attendance periods before using a monthly total. The relevant evidence includes booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Timesheets and working hours still needs sensible review. Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee hours tracking?

The available record can include booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for employee hours tracking?

Timesheets and exports require checking before payroll, accounting or employment use.
