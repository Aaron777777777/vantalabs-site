Quick questions

How does a Panama shift pattern work?

A Panama rota follows a repeating rotation of working and rest days. The important part is identifying where you are in the cycle before adding it to your calendar.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does how panama shifts work manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
