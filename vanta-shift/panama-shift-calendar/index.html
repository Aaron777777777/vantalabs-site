Quick questions

How can I place a Panama rota across future dates?

Enter the repeating Panama work-and-rest sequence and choose the starting point that matches your current position so the rotation lines up across the calendar.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does panama shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
