Quick questions

How can a rota calendar help me plan around night shifts?

Seeing upcoming nights and rest days together can make it easier to understand the pattern ahead and plan your personal time around the rota you expect to work.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does plan life around night shifts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
