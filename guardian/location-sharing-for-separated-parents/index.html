Clearer child location updates across two homes

Where parents share responsibility across two homes, Guardian can give trusted adults access to the same private family information instead of relying on screenshots or repeated messages about a child’s journey.

Useful location context when you genuinely need it

Home and School alerts can provide familiar milestones, while the Family Map and device information are available when either trusted parent needs additional context from the connected child phone.

Access to child-device information does not require the adults to share their own positions with each other. Adult location sharing remains optional, allowing the family to keep child coordination separate from adult tracking.

The adults involved should agree how Guardian will be used and review access when circumstances change. Phone location and notifications can also be delayed or inaccurate, so Guardian should not be treated as an emergency service or a source of perfect location proof.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Can two trusted parents use the same Guardian family information?

Guardian can allow trusted adults with family access to use the available authorised child-device information for everyday coordination.

Do separated parents have to share their own locations with each other?

No. Adult location sharing remains optional even when both adults have access to authorised child-device information.

What can help avoid misunderstandings around shared location access?

Parents can agree how Guardian will be used, who should retain access and when those arrangements need to be reviewed.
