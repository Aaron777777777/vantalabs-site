Connect the worker action with the later review

When digital timesheet system is handled consistently, the resulting record is easier for both workers and authorised managers to understand. For the worker, the expected action is to review recorded hours and flag anything that appears incomplete. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to check the underlying attendance periods before using a monthly total, using booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods alongside the attendance periods from which those hours were calculated. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Timesheets and working hours still needs sensible review. Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for digital timesheet system?

The available record can include booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for digital timesheet system?

Timesheets and exports require checking before payroll, accounting or employment use.
