Plan around how to manage rotating shifts with less guesswork

How to Manage Rotating Shifts keeps the relevant part of your rota close at hand on your own device. It helps you understand a rota before adding it to your personal calendar.

Use pattern order, start dates, shift times, reminders, hours and estimates to keep the calendar aligned with the rota you currently work.

No workplace administration is involved. The app helps you organise information but does not create an official rota.

Quick questions

What is how to manage rotating shifts useful for?

It helps you understand a rota before adding it to your personal calendar using the shift-planning guide stored on your device.

Are calculated totals official records?

No. Hours and earnings shown by the app are personal planning figures based on the information entered.

Does how to manage rotating shifts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
