Family Child Profiles

Organise several children inside one family setup while keeping each child’s tasks, progress and rewards distinct.

Google Play App Store

Keep the family together without mixing progress

A shared household routine should not mean combining everybody’s work into one total. Each child needs to see what belongs to them and what they have already completed.

Family child profiles keep those records separate while giving the parent one place to manage the overall setup. That makes it easier to stay fair and spot what each child needs next.

This gives families separation without fragmenting the routine across multiple accounts. A parent can move between children, compare what still needs attention and update individual responsibilities while the whole system remains part of one family setup.

Each profile keeps its own records. Tasks, approved progress and rewards for one child are not added to another child’s profile.

Quick questions

Can several children have separate profiles in one family setup?

Yes. Each child can have distinct chores, progress and rewards while the parent manages all of the profiles together.

Are family child profiles separate online accounts?

No. They are organisational profiles within the parent-managed setup rather than independent accounts for each child.

What personal details are necessary for family child profiles?

Parents can keep the details limited to what helps their household use the app. A first name or nickname is often enough.
