Where the app helps—and where judgement still matters

Manager and Worker App can remove a great deal of end-of-day guesswork when everyone understands the action expected of them. The worker-facing part should remain simple: use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role. A documented exception route is just as important as the normal flow.

Managers can use workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation. Unusual records still need context before they are corrected or relied upon. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for manager and worker roles?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can an unusual record be reviewed?

Yes. Authorised managers can examine the available timestamps and context, then follow the organisation’s correction process.

What limitation matters most for manager and worker roles?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
