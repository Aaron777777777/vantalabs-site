A focused setup for one connected child profile

Guardian’s core experience supports a connected child profile within a private, parent-managed family. Trusted adults can use the Family Map for the child phone’s latest available location context, receive Home and School place alerts and check useful information such as battery and last-update status when they need to understand what the phone is reporting.

Guardian Premium is intended for families who need support for additional child profiles. Guardian is available on Android through Google Play and on iPhone through the App Store. As with any phone-location service, GPS, connectivity, permissions, buildings, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect updates, so the app should provide context rather than be treated as a guarantee of exact location.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

How many child profiles is this Guardian setup focused on?
