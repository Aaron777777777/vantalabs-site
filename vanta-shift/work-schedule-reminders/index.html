Quick questions

Can I use reminders with the work schedule I enter?

Yes. Reminders can sit alongside the shifts in your personal schedule and help highlight upcoming dates or start times you want to remember.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. Start and finish times can cross midnight so overnight work remains clear in the calendar.

Does work schedule reminders manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
