Guardian vs Life360: what should families compare?

Life360 and Guardian can both help families stay informed about location, but they are not identical products. Guardian concentrates on a private family map, a connected child's latest reported location, Home and School alerts, device status and a child-device SOS option. Whether that is enough depends on which situations you actually need the app to cover.

Where Guardian may be a good fit

  • School-day routines: Home and School arrival or departure alerts can be useful when you mainly want to know that a familiar journey milestone has been reported.
  • Occasional map checks: Parents can see the latest available location together with battery and last-update context, rather than assuming every map pin is live.
  • Adult privacy choices: Adult location sharing is optional. A trusted adult does not have to share their own position merely to participate in the Guardian family.
  • Simple phone-based setup: Guardian works with compatible Android and iPhone devices, without buying a dedicated GPS watch.

What Life360 offers beyond basic family location

According to Life360's UK membership comparison, its free plan includes two Place Alerts, two days of Location History, a Family Driving Summary and Tile item-tracking features. Its paid tiers offer broader location-history, driving and emergency-related benefits. Guardian is not presented as a replacement for those driving, breakdown or emergency membership services; check Life360's current plan details if those are important to you.

Which one should you choose?

Consider Guardian if your priority is a private parent-and-child setup with Home and School alerts, a family map, battery and update context, and optional adult location sharing. Consider Life360's wider plans if driving-related features, item tracking or additional services matter as much as school-day updates. Neither choice can guarantee accurate or uninterrupted phone location.

Price and getting started

Guardian offers a 7-day free trial followed by £3.99/month. Life360 also offers a free plan, but the available features and limits differ. Compare what you would actually use before choosing on price alone. If you want to try Guardian, read the child-device setup guide and location update troubleshooting checks. For a closer look at adult privacy choices, see our family location-sharing guide.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

What Guardian features may matter when looking for a Life360 alternative?
