Quick questions

How can Vanta Shift help with a healthcare rota?

Healthcare workers can organise their own day, night and rest-day pattern and update individual dates when the rota they actually work changes.

Does this require a Vanta Shift account?

No. Vanta Shift is designed as a personal planner without an account or workplace setup.

Does healthcare worker rota manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
