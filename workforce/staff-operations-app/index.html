A practical process for everyday operations

Organisations considering staff operations are usually trying to make a daily worker action easier to review later. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is seeing only the actions and information relevant to their role.

The relevant management view focuses on whether sites, access and worker records match the real organisation. It uses workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system, while preserving the need to check unusual or incomplete records. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents unclear ownership of setup, corrections or ongoing administration from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

What to keep in mind The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for staff operations?

The relevant view can include workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
