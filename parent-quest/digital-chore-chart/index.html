A chore chart that does not get lost

A digital chart gives the family one dependable place to check what needs doing. The current tasks stay together instead of being spread across handwritten lists and reminders.

Parent Quest keeps the experience straightforward: parents create and approve the chores, while children can see and mark the jobs assigned to them.

Keeping the chart on the parent-owned device also means the current version stays in one place. Parents can update chores when something changes instead of trying to keep paper copies, stickers or handwritten lists in sync.

The phone supports the routine. Parent Quest helps organise the chart, but parents still explain, support and supervise the chores where needed.

Quick questions

Who controls the chores on the digital chart?

The parent or guardian controls the chart, including which chores are added, which child receives them and when they are changed.

Can a parent verify digital chore progress?

Yes. Children can mark chores complete, while the parent keeps the final approval step before progress or rewards are confirmed.

Does a digital chore chart remove the need for adult involvement?

No. The digital chart is an organising tool; the parent remains responsible for the child, the chores and any real-world supervision.
