Mobile Time Clock App

Let workers record when work starts and ends from their phones while authorised managers review attendance periods and unusual durations.

Record the start and end of work from a phone

Vanta Workforce gives workers a clear mobile booking-on and booking-off flow so the beginning and end of a working period can be recorded as they happen.

Authorised managers can then review those records for missing booking-off actions, unusual durations or other attendance entries that need checking before totals are relied upon.

Clocking in and out still needs sensible review. A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile time clock?

The available record can include the beginning and end of each recorded attendance period, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for mobile time clock?

A digital timestamp supports review, but it cannot explain every workplace circumstance by itself.
