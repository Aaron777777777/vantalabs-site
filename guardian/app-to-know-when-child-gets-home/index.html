Guardian can provide a Home arrival update when the connected child phone returns to the family’s configured Home safe zone after an ordinary journey.

Quick questions

Do I need to keep the Family Map open to know when the child phone reaches Home?

No. A configured Home safe zone can provide an arrival update without the parent continuously watching the map.

What can I check if a Home arrival update seems late?

The Family Map, battery level and last-update time can provide useful device context, while permissions and battery restrictions may also affect reporting.

Is a child’s Home location shown publicly?

No. Guardian keeps family location information within the private, parent-managed family rather than publishing it through public profiles.
