safe places for kids

Create familiar places and receive useful family alerts

Guardian can treat familiar places such as Home and School as useful location boundaries for a connected child phone, helping families recognise ordinary arrivals and departures.

Google PlayApp Store

A calmer way to stay informed

Instead of asking parents to watch every part of a journey, the place alert can provide the milestone while the private Family Map, battery status and latest available location remain there when extra context is needed.

The connected phone still determines what location information is available. GPS variation, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can delay or change the reported position.

Used openly, these place-based updates can reduce repeated check-in messages while leaving room for trust and independence. They do not guarantee safety or exact positioning, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

How can familiar places help with a child’s everyday routine?

A configured Home or School boundary can provide a useful arrival or leaving update from the connected child phone.

Who can see a child device’s place information in Guardian?

Only trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family can see the connected device’s available location context.

Do safe-place alerts require the parent to share their location?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and is separate from the connected child device’s place alerts.
