The practical thinking behind how to report a workplace incident

How to Report a Workplace Incident is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure.

Authorised managers can then use scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. The technology is strongest when expectations, ownership and correction routes have already been made clear.

Worker safety and scheduled checks still needs sensible review. Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service.

What does Vanta Workforce record for how to report a workplace incident?
