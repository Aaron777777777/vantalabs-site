Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to digital attendance register begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to book on when work begins and book off when the working period ends, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to see whether the record is complete, plausible and ready for review. The relevant evidence includes booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for digital attendance register?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for digital attendance register?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
