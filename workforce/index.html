Common questions

Clear answers about attendance, location and working-hour records.

Can workers review their recorded hours?

Yes. My Hours and timesheet views are designed to show the attendance and working-hour records available to the relevant user.

Does Vanta Workforce guarantee GPS accuracy?

No. Location can be affected by signal, buildings, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour.

Does Vanta Workforce process payroll automatically?

No. Timesheet information and exports should be reviewed before being used in payroll, accounting or employment decisions.
