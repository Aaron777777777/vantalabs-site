Quick questions

How do notifications work with my personal rota?

Notifications can be used around shifts saved in your own calendar to provide personal reminders of upcoming work and unusual start times.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does rota notification app manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
