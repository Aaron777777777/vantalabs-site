Quick questions

Can I use reminders for upcoming night shifts?

Yes. Personal reminders can be used around the night shifts saved in your calendar to help keep unusual starts and upcoming overnight work visible.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does night shift reminders manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
