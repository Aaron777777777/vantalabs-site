From worker action to useful review

Employee timesheet app is most useful when a business wants to replace scattered messages, paper records or end-of-month guesswork with one repeatable process. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is being able to review available hours and identify missing attendance.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess whether the recorded periods accurately describe the hours under review, using My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records and the surrounding workplace context. The app works best inside an explicit operating process. Managers should know how to distinguish a genuine concern from incomplete attendance being mistaken for a final payroll-ready total.
