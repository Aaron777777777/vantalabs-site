Location alerts without following every movement

Location alerts can be more useful than repeatedly opening a map during a familiar journey. Guardian provides Home and School arrival and leaving updates based on the connected child phone, while the private Family Map remains available when a parent needs more context. Battery and last-update information can also help explain what the phone is currently reporting.

These alerts should be treated as helpful phone-location context rather than guaranteed confirmation that a child crossed a boundary at an exact moment. GPS, connectivity, permissions, buildings, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect both the underlying location update and notification timing. Used proportionately, alerts can reduce unnecessary check-ins while leaving room for direct family communication and independence.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
