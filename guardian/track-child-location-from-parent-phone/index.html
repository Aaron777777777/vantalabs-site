Check a connected child phone from the parent device

Guardian lets a parent use their own phone to check useful information from a connected child device without making constant map watching part of the routine.

A calmer way to stay informed

Home and School alerts can provide familiar journey milestones, while the private Family Map, recent location context, battery status and last-update information are available when a parent needs a closer look.

The information reflects what the connected phone has been able to report. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions, battery optimisation and operating-system restrictions can affect both location and update timing.

Used openly and proportionately, those tools can reduce repeated check-in messages while leaving space for ordinary trust and independence. Guardian cannot guarantee perfect accuracy or notification delivery and is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What can a parent check from their own phone in Guardian?

A parent can view available child-device location context, Family Map information, battery status and last-update details inside the private Guardian family.

Does the parent need to leave Guardian open all day?

No. Home and School alerts can provide routine updates while the Family Map is available when additional context is needed.

What if the child phone has stopped updating?

Check the available battery and last-update information and contact the child directly. Permissions, connectivity or battery restrictions may have interrupted reporting.
