family emergency alert

A family alert tool for urgent situations

Guardian provides families with an SOS alert route from a connected child device to trusted adults in the private Guardian family. It is designed to help the family recognise that the child is asking for attention and coordinate a response.

Google PlayApp Store

An extra way to reach trusted adults

The alert can include the latest available location and device context from the child phone. That information may help adults decide who is closest, who should call the child and whether another form of assistance is needed.

A useful family response plan should not depend on one notification. Parents can decide in advance which adults are likely to respond, how they will communicate with each other and what circumstances require action outside Guardian.

Guardian cannot guarantee delivery of an SOS notification or perfect phone location. It is not an emergency service and does not replace direct contact with emergency services when somebody may be in immediate danger.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Who can receive family SOS information in Guardian?

SOS information stays within the private Guardian family for trusted adults with access rather than being placed on a public profile.

Can the SOS alert help family adults decide who should respond?

Yes. Available location and device context can provide useful information while trusted adults coordinate who should contact the child or take further action.

Should a family rely only on Guardian for an emergency response?

No. Notification delivery cannot be guaranteed, and Guardian is not an emergency service or a substitute for direct emergency contact.
