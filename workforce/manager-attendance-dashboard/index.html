A practical process for everyday operations

Manager attendance dashboard becomes valuable when workers and managers need the same understanding of what was recorded, when it happened and what still needs checking. The worker-facing process centres on having relevant attendance and check actions reflected accurately. It should remain understandable during busy arrivals, departures, site moves and scheduled checks.

For an authorised manager, the useful question is which workers are active and which records require attention. Vanta Workforce keeps worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks together so the review does not depend on collecting separate updates. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review making decisions from stale, incomplete or misunderstood device data proportionately.

What to keep in mind Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for manager attendance dashboard?

The relevant view can include worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
