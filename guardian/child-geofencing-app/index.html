Child geofencing for useful arrival and leaving alerts

Child geofencing in Guardian is built around useful milestones rather than constant map watching. A connected child phone can support location boundaries around familiar places such as Home and School.

Google PlayApp Store

A calmer way to stay informed

When the phone moves into or out of one of those configured areas, Guardian can provide an arrival or leaving update to trusted adults in the private family. The Family Map remains available when the alert needs more context.

A geofence is based on phone location rather than an exact physical checkpoint. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions, battery optimisation and background restrictions can all affect when a boundary event is detected.

Adult location sharing is optional, and Guardian cannot guarantee perfectly timed alerts or exact positioning. It supports ordinary family communication but is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What does child geofencing mean in Guardian?

It means using the connected child phone’s permitted location to recognise when it enters or leaves a configured area such as Home or School.

Can geofence alerts be delayed by the child phone?

Yes. GPS conditions, connectivity, location permissions, battery optimisation and background restrictions can affect detection and alert timing.

Does a parent have to share their own location to use child geofencing?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and is separate from the connected child device’s safe-zone features.
