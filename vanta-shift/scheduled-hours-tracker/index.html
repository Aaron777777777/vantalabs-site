Quick questions

What does the scheduled hours tracker show?

It helps you review the hours created by the shifts currently saved in your personal calendar, including changes you make to individual dates.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does scheduled hours tracker manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
