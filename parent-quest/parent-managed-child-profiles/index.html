Give children their own view without giving up control

A child profile can make the experience feel personal without becoming an independent account. The child sees the tasks and progress intended for them, while the parent manages the wider setup.

Protected controls remain behind the Parent PIN, and everything stays on the parent-owned device. That keeps the family routine simple and appropriately adult managed.

The child profile can therefore feel personal without giving the child control over the family settings behind it. Parents decide which chores appear, review submitted progress and manage rewards while children interact with the simpler view intended for them.

The parent owns the setup. Children do not create accounts or independently control their profile settings.

Quick questions

What does the parent manage inside each child profile?

The parent manages the child’s chores, approval, progress and rewards while the child uses the simpler routine built for them.

Is a parent-managed child profile a child account?

No. It is a profile within the adult-managed family setup rather than an independent account owned or controlled by the child.

How much personal data does a parent-managed profile need?

Parents should enter only what is useful to their family. A simple name or nickname can be enough to identify the profile.
