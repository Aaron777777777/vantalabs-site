Quick questions

What can I keep in my personal work rota?

You can keep working dates, shift times, days off, notes and repeating patterns together in the personal rota stored in Vanta Shift.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does work rota app manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
