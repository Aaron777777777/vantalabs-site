Use one parent device for the whole family

Younger children often do not need their own phone, email address or app account. They simply need a clear way to see what they are expected to do.

Parent Quest keeps each child’s tasks and progress separate while the parent manages everything from one family setup. That makes the system easier to introduce and easier to supervise.

For younger children especially, that can remove unnecessary setup. They can have a clear personalised chore list without being expected to own a phone, manage credentials or look after a separate app account.

Children do not need separate accounts. Child profiles sit inside the parent-managed app rather than becoming independent user accounts.

Quick questions

Is a separate device required for every child’s chore list?

No. The parent device can hold separate profiles and chore lists for multiple children.

Do children need accounts to keep their chores separate?

No. Parent Quest separates chores through child profiles rather than independent email-based accounts.

Who manages the chore system on the parent device?

The parent or guardian manages protected settings, approvals and rewards for the family.
