A simpler way to compare family location tools

Guardian combines the private Family Map with Home and School alerts so trusted adults can receive familiar journey milestones and then look at the map only when they need additional phone-location context.

Quick questions

What makes a family locator useful for ordinary routines?

Useful place alerts, a clear Family Map and device context can answer routine questions without requiring adults to monitor every movement.

Should every adult have to share their own location in a family locator?

That depends on the service. In Guardian, adult location sharing is optional and separate from authorised child-device information.

Why does device context matter when comparing family locator apps?

Battery and update information can help explain whether a map position may be old because the connected phone has not reported recently.
