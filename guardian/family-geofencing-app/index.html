family geofencing

Family geofencing without the technical clutter

Family geofencing gives Guardian a way to recognise meaningful arrivals and departures around familiar places without asking parents to follow every movement on a map.

Google PlayApp Store

A calmer way to stay informed

Home and School can act as useful routine boundaries for a connected child phone. Trusted adults can receive place-based updates and then open the private Family Map only when they need additional context.

The boundary event depends on location reports from the phone. Weak signal, GPS variation, permissions, battery-saving behaviour and operating-system restrictions can all change when an update is detected.

Family information stays inside the parent-managed Guardian family rather than a public profile or social map. Geofencing provides useful context, not guaranteed tracking, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Does family geofencing require parents to watch the Family Map?

No. Place-based arrival and leaving alerts are intended to cover useful milestones while the map remains available for occasional context.

What phone settings can affect a geofence event?

Location permissions, battery optimisation, background restrictions, connectivity and GPS conditions can all influence when the connected phone reports.

Is family geofencing information public in Guardian?

No. Location information remains inside the private, parent-managed Guardian family for trusted adults with access.
