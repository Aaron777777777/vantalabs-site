Chore App on a Parent Phone

Run the family chore routine from your own phone, with tasks, approvals and rewards kept together in one place.

Google Play App Store

Manage the routine from the phone you already use

A family should not need a separate device for every child just to organise a few household jobs. Parent Quest is built around a parent-owned phone that can be shared when a child needs to view their list.

You keep control of the profiles, chores, approvals, reward coins and custom rewards. Children can take part in the routine without needing their own account or login.

The parent owns and manages the setup. Protected settings and approvals remain behind the Parent PIN on the parent’s device.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
