Share useful child safety updates with another trusted parent

Guardian can give another trusted parent or caregiver access to the same private family information when responsibility for a child is shared. That can reduce the need to send screenshots or manually forward routine location updates.

Give another trusted parent access to the same family

A trusted adult with family access can use the authorised child-device information available through Guardian, including relevant map context and family alerts, instead of relying on another parent to relay every update.

Giving somebody access should still be a deliberate family decision. Only people who are genuinely trusted should be included, and access should be reviewed if caregiving arrangements or family circumstances change.

Adding another trusted adult does not require either adult to share their own location. Adult sharing remains optional, while child-device location can still be affected by normal phone and GPS limitations. Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Can another trusted parent access the same child-device information in Guardian?

A trusted adult who has been given access to the private Guardian family can use the authorised child-device information available to that family.

Does sharing child information mean parents must send screenshots to each other?

No. Family access can let trusted adults use the available Guardian information directly instead of repeatedly forwarding screenshots or updates.

Does giving another parent access force either adult to share their own location?

No. Adult location sharing remains optional even when both trusted adults have access to authorised child-device information.
