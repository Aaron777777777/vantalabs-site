Build a clearer view of warehouse worker rota

The aim of Warehouse Worker Rota is simple: make the next working dates easier to see. It helps you organise irregular work without accounts or workplace administration.

You remain free to edit rotating shifts, nights, weekends, rest days, hours and earnings estimates rather than being locked into the first version of the rota.

Designed for the individual worker. Vanta Shift is for the individual using the app, not for managing other people.

Quick questions

How can Vanta Shift help with a warehouse rota?

Warehouse workers can keep rotating shifts, nights, weekends and rest days together while tracking the scheduled hours in their own rota.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does warehouse worker rota manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
