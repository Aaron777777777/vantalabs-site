Support independence without watching every step

When a child begins walking to School independently, families often want reassurance around a few agreed moments rather than constant observation. Guardian can provide a Home departure update, a School arrival alert and access to the latest available Family Map location when extra context is needed.

That approach leaves room for independence while keeping trusted adults informed about familiar milestones. GPS and notification delivery can still be affected by the phone, network and surroundings, so Guardian should support family communication rather than replace it. In immediate danger, contact the child and emergency services directly.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Can Guardian help when my child walks to School?

Use Home and School alerts to support children who walk to School without watching their location continuously.

Can phone settings affect Guardian location updates?

Yes. GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions can affect location reporting and notification timing.

Is Guardian an emergency service?

No. Guardian provides family location and alert context. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
