Turning daily activity into a usable record

Field staff hours becomes valuable when workers and managers need the same understanding of what was recorded, when it happened and what still needs checking. Workers mainly need a consistent attendance process from a supported mobile device. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

For an authorised manager, the useful question is how attendance is recorded across changing sites and field locations. Vanta Workforce keeps off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history together so the review does not depend on collecting separate updates. A sensible rollout is to define the expected action, show workers how it works and agree who reviews exceptions. This matters because weak connectivity or unclear expectations producing inconsistent records can still occur during normal use.
