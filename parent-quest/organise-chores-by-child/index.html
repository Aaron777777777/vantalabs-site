Put every chore in the right place

Clear ownership removes one of the most common arguments around household jobs: nobody knowing who was meant to do what. Assigning tasks to a child profile makes the expectation visible.

Parents can then review progress child by child rather than trying to untangle a shared checklist. The result is easier to manage and easier for children to follow.

Assign chores clearly. Each task should sit with the child responsible for completing it.

Quick questions

Can each child have their own profile?

Yes. Each child can have separate chores, progress and rewards.

Do child profiles need separate accounts?

No. They remain part of the parent-managed family setup.

How much personal information is needed?

Parents should only enter what the family needs. A first name or nickname is often enough.
