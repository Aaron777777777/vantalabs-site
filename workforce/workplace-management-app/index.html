Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to workplace management begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation. The relevant evidence includes workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction. For Workplace Management App, a short written process can prevent the same question being handled differently by different managers.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workplace management?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for workplace management?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
