One calm notification when School is reached

A School arrival alert gives parents one useful update at the point they usually care about most: when the connected child phone reaches the School safe zone.

Google PlayApp Store

A simple answer to one school-run question

Guardian can notify trusted adults when that phone crosses into the family’s configured School area. The alert reduces the need to keep refreshing the Family Map during an ordinary school journey.

The alert describes the connected phone crossing a location boundary; it does not prove that a child has entered a particular classroom or building. GPS, signal and background phone settings can affect timing.

For a regular school run, that distinction matters. The arrival alert can provide useful reassurance while battery level, last-update time and the Family Map remain available when a parent needs more context. Guardian is not an emergency service; urgent concerns still require direct contact and the appropriate emergency response.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Who receives a Guardian School arrival update?

Only trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family can see the child device information and related arrival context.

What does the School arrival alert actually confirm?

It indicates that the connected phone has crossed into the configured School safe zone; it does not confirm entry into a particular classroom.

Do parents have to share their own location to use School alerts?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and is not required for the child device’s School arrival alert.
