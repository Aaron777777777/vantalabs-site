The practical thinking behind troubleshooting mobile attendance

Troubleshooting Mobile Attendance is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role.

Authorised managers can then use workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for troubleshooting mobile attendance?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for troubleshooting mobile attendance?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
