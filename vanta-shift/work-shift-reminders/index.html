Quick questions

Can I add reminders around individual work shifts?

Yes. You can use personal reminders with saved shifts to help keep their scheduled dates and start times in view.

Does this require a Vanta Shift account?

No. Vanta Shift is designed as a personal planner without an account or workplace setup.

Does work shift reminders manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
