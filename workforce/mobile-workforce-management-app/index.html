Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile workforce management?

The available record can include off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for mobile workforce management?

The organisation still needs a fallback process for weak signal, device problems and unclear site information.
