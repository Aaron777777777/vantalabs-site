How to Estimate Shift Pay

Use your saved shifts and the pay details you enter to form a personal estimate of upcoming earnings and see how different working weeks contribute to that figure.

Google Play App Store

Build a clearer view of how to estimate shift pay

The aim of How to Estimate Shift Pay is simple: make the next working dates easier to see. It helps you understand a rota before adding it to your personal calendar.

You remain free to edit pattern order, start dates, shift times, reminders, hours and estimates rather than being locked into the first version of the rota.

Designed for the individual worker. The app helps you organise information but does not create an official rota.

Quick questions

How can I estimate pay from my shifts?

Enter the shifts and pay details you want to track and Vanta Shift can turn them into a personal earnings estimate alongside your rota.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does how to estimate shift pay manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
