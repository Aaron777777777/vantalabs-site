Parent-managed access with clear family boundaries

Guardian places family access under trusted adult control. A connected child device shares information only inside its Guardian family, and parents decide which trusted adults can access that family.

The app combines map context, Home and School alerts, device status and SOS tools without public profiles or a social feed. Adult location sharing is optional rather than a requirement.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Who can see information used for parent child location?

Only trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family.

Does Guardian show battery and last-update time?

Yes. Those details help explain whether the child phone is reporting normally.

Is parent location sharing required?

No. Adult location sharing is optional.
