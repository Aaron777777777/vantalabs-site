Guardian keeps connected child-device information within the authorised family instead of placing a child profile into a public or searchable social network. Trusted adults with access can use that information for ordinary family coordination.

Quick questions

What does private child location tracking mean in Guardian?

Connected child-device information stays within the authorised Guardian family rather than being placed on a public or searchable social profile.

Does private tracking mean the child phone always reports an exact position?

No. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can affect both location accuracy and update timing.

Should children know that Guardian location features are being used?

Families should explain why location sharing is being used, which trusted adults can access it and how it fits into everyday routines.
