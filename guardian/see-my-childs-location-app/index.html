A quick location check when plans change

When a pickup is late or a familiar journey changes, Guardian lets a trusted adult check the connected child phone’s latest available location. The map also shows battery status and when the phone last reported.

This page is about the direct task of checking the map, rather than watching a journey continuously. Home and School alerts can handle many routine updates without repeated manual checks.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Is Guardian available on iPhone and Android?

Guardian is available on Android through Google Play. The iPhone version is awaiting App Store approval.

Can see my child's location ever be inaccurate?

Yes. GPS, signal, buildings, permissions and battery settings can affect location and timing.

Is Guardian an emergency service?

No. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
