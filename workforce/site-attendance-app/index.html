What the organisation actually needs to see

The question behind site attendance is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is knowing that the expected booking action has been received.

The relevant management view focuses on who is currently recorded as working at the relevant workplace. It uses the latest available worker status connected to attendance history, while preserving the need to check unusual or incomplete records. Good records begin before anyone opens the app. The organisation should set clear expectations and establish how confusing delayed device information with a definite absence will be handled.

What to keep in mind Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for site attendance?

The relevant view can include the latest available worker status connected to attendance history, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.
