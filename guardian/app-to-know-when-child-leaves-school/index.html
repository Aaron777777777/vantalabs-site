A clear update when the child phone leaves School

A School departure alert gives parents a useful indication that the connected child phone has moved outside the configured School safe zone at the end of the school day.

Useful school-run updates, without watching the map

That update can mark the beginning of the journey away from School without requiring the parent to watch every movement on the Family Map.

Leaving the safe zone does not mean the child has reached Home, a pickup point or another destination. GPS, signal and phone settings can also affect when the boundary crossing is reported.

Parents can use a later Home arrival alert as another familiar milestone and open the Family Map, battery status or last-update information when the journey needs a closer check.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What does a Guardian School departure alert confirm?

It indicates that the connected child phone has moved outside the configured School safe zone; it does not confirm the next destination.

Why might a School leaving alert be delayed?

Buildings, GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can all affect background location reporting and alert timing.

Is a School departure notification a substitute for emergency contact?

No. Guardian is not an emergency service. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
