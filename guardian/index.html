Guardian · Family Tracker

Private family location, Home and School alerts and SOS

Get a useful update when they leave Home, arrive at School or make it back safely. Guardian keeps private family location sharing, device status and SOS close when your family needs them.

Google Play App Store

The four updates that matter during an ordinary school day

Guardian can let trusted adults know when a connected child leaves Home, arrives at School, leaves School and returns Home. Open the private Family Map when you need more context instead of watching a public or social location feed.

Recent location history, battery level and last-update time help explain what the child device is reporting. An SOS option is available when something is wrong, while adult location sharing remains optional.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.

Quick questions

Who can see a child’s location?

Only trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family can see the available family location information.

Is parent location sharing required?

No. Adult location sharing remains optional.

Can location or alerts ever be delayed?

Yes. GPS, buildings, signal, permissions, battery restrictions and device conditions can affect what Guardian receives.
