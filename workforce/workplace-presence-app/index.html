A practical process for everyday operations

Organisations considering workplace presence are usually trying to make a daily worker action easier to review later. Workers mainly need knowing that the expected booking action has been received. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

The manager view is intended to make who is currently recorded as working at the relevant workplace easier to review. It presents the latest available worker status connected to attendance history without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review confusing delayed device information with a definite absence proportionately.

What to keep in mind Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workplace presence?

The relevant view can include the latest available worker status connected to attendance history, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
