Connect the worker action with the later review

When workplace attendance register is handled consistently, the resulting record is easier for both workers and authorised managers to understand. For the worker, the expected action is to complete the expected attendance action so their current status can update. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to see who is presently recorded as working and where follow-up may be needed, using current booking status, workplace and recent attendance activity together with the dates, workplaces and workers represented in the register. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Workplace presence still needs sensible review. Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workplace attendance register?

The available record can include current booking status, workplace and recent attendance activity, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for workplace attendance register?

Current status can be delayed by connectivity, permissions or the condition of the worker’s device.
