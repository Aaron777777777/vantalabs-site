Quick questions

What is employee shift alerts useful for?

It helps you keep unusual starts and changing working days easier to remember using the shift reminder stored on your device.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does employee shift alerts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
