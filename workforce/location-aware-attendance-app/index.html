A clearer record for the real working day

Location-Aware Attendance App should solve a recognisable workplace problem rather than simply move an old form onto a phone. The worker-facing part should remain simple: allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking. A documented exception route is just as important as the normal flow.

Managers can use booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace. Unusual records still need context before they are corrected or relied upon. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for location-aware attendance?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can an unusual record be reviewed?

Yes. Authorised managers can examine the available timestamps and context, then follow the organisation’s correction process.

What limitation matters most for location-aware attendance?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
