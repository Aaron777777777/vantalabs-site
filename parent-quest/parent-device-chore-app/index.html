Use one parent device for the whole family

Younger children often do not need their own phone, email address or app account. They simply need a clear way to see what they are expected to do.

Parent Quest keeps each child’s tasks and progress separate while the parent manages everything from one family setup. That makes the system easier to introduce and easier to supervise.

Children do not need separate accounts. Child profiles sit inside the parent-managed app rather than becoming independent user accounts.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
