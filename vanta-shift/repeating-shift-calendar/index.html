Quick questions

What is repeating shift calendar useful for?

It helps you place a complete working cycle across the calendar quickly using the repeating shift pattern stored on your device.

Can I use this with rotating work?

Yes. The calendar supports repeating day, night and off patterns as well as individually entered shifts.

Does repeating shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
