Place alerts for the moments that matter

Guardian safe zones create simple virtual boundaries around familiar places such as Home and School. Parents can receive useful arrival and leaving alerts without following every movement on a map, making the feature better suited to ordinary routines.

Safe-zone timing depends on the connected phone’s available location readings and can be affected by buildings, weak signal, permissions or battery restrictions. The alerts provide helpful context, but they should not be treated as perfectly immediate or guaranteed.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Does family place alerts mean watching the map all day?

No. Guardian focuses on useful alerts and quick checks when context is needed.

Can phone settings delay updates?

Yes. Battery optimisation, permissions and background restrictions can affect reporting.

Does Guardian have public profiles?

No. Family information stays inside a private, parent-managed account.
