Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to worker phone checks begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. The relevant evidence includes scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction. The practical test for Worker Phone Checks is whether someone unfamiliar with the original event can still understand the record later.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for worker phone checks?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for worker phone checks?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
