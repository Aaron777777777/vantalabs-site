Quick questions

Can I review estimated earnings week by week?

Yes. The shifts and pay details you enter can be used to show a personal estimated total for each working week.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does weekly earnings tracker manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
