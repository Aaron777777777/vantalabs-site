Quick questions

Can I use Vanta Shift for a repeating day-and-night rota?

Yes. You can keep day shifts, night shifts and rest days in the same repeating personal rota and adjust individual dates when the normal cycle changes.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does day and night shifts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
