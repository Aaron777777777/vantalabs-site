Parent-Approved Rewards

Keep rewards connected to work you have reviewed rather than allowing every completed-task tap to count automatically.

Google Play App Store

Make approval part of the reward routine

Rewards feel fairer when children know the same rule applies each time: finish the task, let the parent check it and then receive the agreed progress.

That small approval step stops unfinished jobs from adding reward coins and gives parents room to use their judgement when effort, age or circumstances need to be considered.

It also helps keep rewards tied to the family’s own rules rather than to a button press. A parent can recognise genuine effort, ask for something to be finished properly or decide that a particular chore should count differently without the app making that judgement automatically.

The parent confirms reward progress. Children can submit tasks, but they cannot approve their own coins or rewards.

Quick questions

Why are reward-related approvals protected by the Parent PIN?

The PIN keeps the decision that affects confirmed progress and rewards with the adult managing the family setup.

Can a child approve the work that leads to a reward?

No. Children can mark chores complete, but the parent or guardian decides when that work is approved.

Does approving progress towards a reward require lots of steps?

No. Parent Quest is designed around a quick review so the adult can confirm the work without adding a complicated process to the family routine.
