Guardian location not updating? Try these checks

When a Guardian location does not update, start with the device context rather than assuming the child has stopped moving. Check the connected phone’s last-update information and battery status to see how recently Guardian appears to have received a report.

Six checks when Guardian shows an old location

An old pin on the Family Map means Guardian may not have received a more recent location from the child phone. It does not mean the child is still at that spot. Start by reading the displayed last-update time and device status.

  1. Check the timestamp: Is the reported location minutes or hours old? Read the last-update information before interpreting the map.
  2. Check the child phone's connection: Confirm it is switched on and has working mobile data or Wi-Fi. A poor signal can delay reporting.
  3. Check location services: On the child device, review system location settings and Guardian's location permissions, including background access where available.
  4. Check battery-saving restrictions: Low-power modes or manufacturer-specific background limits may reduce updates. Review the phone's app and battery settings.
  5. Check the app: If the phone is available, open Guardian and check whether it reports normally. Restarting the app or phone may help with a temporary device problem.
  6. Test again: Look at the next reported timestamp and check Home or School notifications with the child; do not assume one successful update guarantees continuous reporting.

When to contact your child directly

If plans have changed or you need to know where your child is now, contact them or another trusted adult. Do not wait for a map refresh in an urgent situation; Guardian is not an emergency service.

If you're just getting started, follow our child-device setup guide. For more context about timestamps and battery readings, see battery and last-update status.

Quick questions

What should I check first when Guardian location stops updating?

Check the connected phone’s last-update information and battery status to see how recently Guardian appears to have received a device report.

Which phone conditions can stop a newer location reaching Guardian?

Connectivity, location permissions, background restrictions and battery-saving behaviour can all delay or interrupt normal reporting.

Should I wait for the map if the location is important?

No. If you genuinely need to know what is happening, contact the family member directly rather than relying only on a location refresh.
