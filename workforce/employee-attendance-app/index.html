A simple attendance flow for employees and managers

Employees can book on when work starts and book off when it ends without needing access to management tools. Their recorded attendance then becomes available to authorised managers for review.

Managers can see booking times, recorded duration, workplace information and attendance history, giving them a clearer starting point when checking whether a working record is complete.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee attendance app?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for employee attendance app?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
