Making the workplace process easier to follow

The practical purpose of mobile time clock is not simply to digitise a form. It is to produce a clearer record that remains useful after the working day has finished. From the worker’s perspective, success means a repeatable booking-on and booking-off routine. A fair exception route should also exist for genuine mistakes and device problems.

The manager view is intended to make when attendance began, when it ended and whether anything is missing easier to review. It presents the start and end points of each recorded attendance period without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review missed book-off actions, late entries or incorrect booking times proportionately.

What to keep in mind A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile time clock?

The relevant view can include the start and end points of each recorded attendance period, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
