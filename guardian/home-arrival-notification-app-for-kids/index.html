Home arrival notifications for ordinary family routines

A Home arrival notification can give parents a simple update when the connected child phone returns inside the family’s configured Home safe zone.

Google PlayApp Store

Clear Home updates when a journey begins or ends

For everyday trips such as the journey back from School or another familiar activity, that can reduce repeated check-in messages while keeping the routine easy to understand.

If more context is needed, the parent can check the Family Map, battery level and last-update time. GPS, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions may affect when an arrival is reported.

The notification describes a location event from the phone rather than guaranteeing an exact moment at the front door. Families should still communicate directly when plans change or an expected update does not appear. Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What is a Guardian Home arrival notification based on?

It is based on the connected child phone entering the family’s configured Home safe zone.

Who can see the Home arrival information?

Only trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family can see the connected child device’s location context.

What if the expected Home arrival notification does not appear?

Parents can check the latest available location and device status and contact the child directly; phone settings or connectivity may delay reporting.
