Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

A useful staff clock-in app process begins with the real workplace routine, not with the software screen. The app supplies the mobile action; the business supplies the expectation. Here that means a repeatable booking-on and booking-off routine, together with a known support route.

Authorised managers can use the available record to examine when attendance began, when it ended and whether anything is missing. That evidence includes the start and end points of each recorded attendance period and should be read with its timing and workplace circumstances. A sensible rollout is to define the expected action, show workers how it works and agree who reviews exceptions. This matters because missed book-off actions, late entries or incorrect booking times can still occur during normal use.

What to keep in mind A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

What does Vanta Workforce record for staff clock-in app?
