A practical process for everyday operations

Field worker attendance can bring structure to a process that otherwise depends heavily on memory, messages or manual reconciliation. For the worker, the important part is a consistent attendance process from a supported mobile device. The organisation should explain what counts as completion and what to do when a device, account or connection problem prevents the normal action.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess how attendance is recorded across changing sites and field locations, using off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history and the surrounding workplace context. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review weak connectivity or unclear expectations producing inconsistent records proportionately.
