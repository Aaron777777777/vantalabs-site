Personal Task Lists for Children

Give each child a personal list that reflects their own age, routine and responsibilities.

Google Play App Store

One child’s list does not need to match another’s

Siblings may be ready for very different jobs. Personal lists make those differences clear without comparing one child’s progress with another’s.

The parent can manage every profile from the same device while each child sees a simpler view of their own tasks.

Personal lists also make it easier to change expectations without affecting a sibling’s routine. One child might need a short morning checklist while another has after-school jobs, and both can remain part of the same parent-managed family setup.

Fair does not always mean identical. Parents can choose different expectations for children of different ages and abilities.

Quick questions

Can siblings have completely different personal task lists?

Yes. Each child profile can have its own tasks, allowing parents to reflect different ages, routines and responsibilities without combining everything into one list.

Who manages each child’s personal list?

The parent or guardian manages every list from the parent-owned device and can change tasks independently for each child.

Does Parent Quest choose tasks based on a child’s age?

No. The app does not decide what is age-appropriate; the parent chooses tasks that fit the individual child and household.
