Understand what real-time family location actually means

Guardian treats real-time family location as current phone-based context rather than a promise that every family device is broadcasting continuously. The private Family Map shows the latest location information Guardian has received from connected devices.

Quick questions

Does real-time family location mean every Guardian device updates continuously?

No. Guardian shows available phone-location information, and reporting frequency depends on each device’s connectivity, permissions, background activity and battery conditions.

Do parents have to share their own location to use family location features?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and can remain off independently of authorised child-device location features.

Why are device conditions important when looking at the Family Map?

A phone with weak connectivity, restricted background activity or battery limitations may not have reported as recently as another device.
