Quick questions

What is work time summary useful for?

It helps you understand how the shifts in your week or month are adding up using the scheduled-hours view stored on your device.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does work time summary manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
