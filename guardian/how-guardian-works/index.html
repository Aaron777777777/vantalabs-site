Guardian works by connecting a child phone to a private, parent-managed family so trusted adults can use the location and device information that phone is able to provide.

How to get Guardian working for your family

Guardian uses a private family setup and a compatible phone carried by the child. Parents can check the latest available location and receive Home and School alerts when the connected device reports the relevant movement. Here's a practical way to get started and check that it suits your routine.

1. Install Guardian and set up the family

Download Guardian from Google Play or the App Store using the links above. Follow the app's family setup instructions and decide which trusted adults should have access. Adults can choose whether to share their own location; it is not required for child-device tracking.

2. Connect the child's phone

Follow the child-device setup guide on the phone the child will actually carry. Check the phone's location permissions and connectivity, including background location access where the operating system offers it.

3. Configure Home and School alerts

Add the relevant Home and School safe zones, then test an arrival and a departure with the child. Notifications depend on the connected phone and its permissions, so don't assume the first configuration always works perfectly.

4. Read the last-update time as well as the map

The Family Map shows the latest available reported position, not a guarantee of continuous live tracking. Battery level and last-update information help you interpret older reports. If it stops updating, start with the location update checks.

5. Agree how your family will use it

Explain to children when parents plan to check location and what the alerts mean. For an important change of plan, speak directly with your child or another trusted adult. Guardian supports family communication but cannot guarantee location accuracy, notification delivery or safety. It is not an emergency service.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What does the connected child phone do in Guardian?

It provides the available child-device location and status information that Guardian can use within the private family.

How do Home and School alerts fit into how Guardian works?

They use available phone-location information around configured places to provide useful routine arrival or leaving milestones.

Why can Guardian information sometimes be delayed?

GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions, background restrictions, battery behaviour and operating-system limits can all affect when the connected phone reports.
