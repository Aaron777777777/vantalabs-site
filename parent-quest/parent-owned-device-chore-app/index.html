Let children use the routine on your device while keeping account settings and approvals under adult control.

Share the screen without sharing the controls

A parent-owned device can be handed over briefly for a child to check tasks or mark something complete. That should not give them access to protected settings.

The Parent PIN separates the everyday child view from approvals and management tools. You can share the useful part of the app without handing over control of the family system.

Keep the Parent PIN to yourself. The protected area should remain accessible only to the parent or guardian managing the routine.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
