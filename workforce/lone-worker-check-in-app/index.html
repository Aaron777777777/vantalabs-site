What the organisation should expect from the process

Businesses normally consider lone worker check-in because the current process is slow to check, difficult to consolidate or too dependent on memory. A clear process tells the worker to respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. The relevant evidence includes scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for lone worker check-in?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for lone worker check-in?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
