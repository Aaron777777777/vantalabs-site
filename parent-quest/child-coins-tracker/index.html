Keep a clear record of approved progress

It is easy to lose track when tasks happen across several days and rewards take time to reach. A visible balance gives the parent and child the same answer about what has already been approved.

The tracker sits alongside the child’s own tasks and rewards, so their progress stays organised in one profile rather than being mixed into a general family total.

That separate balance gives parents a clearer record when siblings are working towards different goals. Only progress the parent has approved needs to appear in the total, and the balance remains an in-app family measure rather than anything with monetary value.

The tracker records in-app coins only. It is not a bank balance, savings account or record of real-money payments.

Quick questions

Does the balance in a child coins tracker represent real money?

No. The balance is an in-app record of approved progress and has no cash value.

Who decides what adds to a child’s coin balance?

The parent decides which tasks can contribute reward coins, the values used and when submitted work should be approved.

Can tracked reward coins be bought, withdrawn or exchanged through Vanta Labs?

No. Vanta Labs does not sell reward coins, and the coins cannot be withdrawn or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
