Quick questions

What can shift notifications remind me about?

They can provide personal reminders around the shifts you have saved, helping make upcoming dates and unusual start times easier to notice.

Can I change the information later?

Yes. Shifts, times, notes and patterns can be adjusted when your real schedule changes.

Does shift notification app manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
