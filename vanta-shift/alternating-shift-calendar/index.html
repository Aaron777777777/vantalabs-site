Quick questions

How can I keep an alternating shift pattern clear?

Set up the repeating sequence you work and keep it in one personal calendar, then adjust individual dates whenever your real rota differs from the normal pattern.

Can I add notes to a shift?

Yes. Notes can be kept with a shift so useful personal details remain beside the date and time.

Does alternating shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
