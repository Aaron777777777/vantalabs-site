The practical thinking behind how gps clock-in works

How GPS Clock-In Works is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking.

Authorised managers can then use booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace. The technology is strongest when expectations, ownership and correction routes have already been made clear.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for how gps clock-in works?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for how gps clock-in works?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
