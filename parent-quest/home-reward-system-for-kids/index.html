Create a system that fits real family life

Home routines change from week to week. School days, weekends and holidays can all need different expectations, so a rigid reward plan quickly becomes more trouble than it is worth.

With Parent Quest, the parent controls the tasks, approvals, coin values and reward choices. That keeps the routine flexible while still giving children a clear view of what they can work towards.

The household sets the rules. Parent Quest organises your chosen system rather than imposing fixed chores, coin amounts or rewards.

Quick questions

Who creates the rewards?

The parent or guardian creates and provides every reward.

Are rewards supplied by Vanta Labs?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee family rewards.

Can rewards be changed?

Yes. Parents can change the reward list as the family’s interests and circumstances change.
