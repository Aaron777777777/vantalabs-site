Working Hours Tracker is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should review recorded hours and flag anything that appears incomplete.

The management side is about being able to check the underlying attendance periods before using a monthly total. That judgement is supported by booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for working hours tracking?

The available record can include booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for working hours tracking?

Timesheets and exports require checking before payroll, accounting or employment use.
