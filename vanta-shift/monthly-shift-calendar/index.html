Keep the details behind monthly shift calendar together

Rotating work can be difficult to picture when dates and times are spread across different places. It helps you see working days, rest days and changing times together.

Quick questions

What is monthly shift calendar useful for?

It helps you see working days, rest days and changing times together using the personal shift calendar stored on your device.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does monthly shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
