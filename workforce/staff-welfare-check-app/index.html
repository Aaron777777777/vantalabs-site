Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for staff welfare checks?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for staff welfare checks?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
