The practical thinking behind how scheduled worker prompts work

How Scheduled Worker Prompts Work is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure.

Authorised managers can then use scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected.
