Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

Organisations considering digital clock-in are usually trying to make a daily worker action easier to review later. For the worker, the important part is a repeatable booking-on and booking-off routine. The organisation should explain what counts as completion and what to do when a device, account or connection problem prevents the normal action.

For an authorised manager, the useful question is when attendance began, when it ended and whether anything is missing. Vanta Workforce keeps the start and end points of each recorded attendance period together so the review does not depend on collecting separate updates. Good records begin before anyone opens the app. The organisation should set clear expectations and establish how missed book-off actions, late entries or incorrect booking times will be handled.

What to keep in mind A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for digital clock-in?

The relevant view can include the start and end points of each recorded attendance period, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.
