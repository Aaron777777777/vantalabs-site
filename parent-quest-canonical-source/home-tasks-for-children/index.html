Home Tasks for Children

Choose useful jobs children can help with at home, from putting things away to helping prepare for the next day.

Google Play App Store

Help children take part in everyday home life

Home tasks give children a practical way to contribute. The job might be small, but completing it regularly can still build confidence and responsibility.

The parent chooses what is safe and suitable, shows the child what to do and checks the result afterwards.

Parents remain responsible for safety. Children should only be given tasks that suit their age, ability and level of supervision.

Quick questions

Can each child have a different task list?

Yes. Parents can organise separate tasks for different child profiles.

Who creates the tasks?

The parent or guardian creates and manages the tasks.

Does the app decide whether a task is suitable?

No. The parent remains responsible for choosing age-appropriate tasks.
