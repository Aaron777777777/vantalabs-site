Chore Reward App

Connect everyday chores to simple family rewards without turning the whole household into a points competition.

Google Play App Store

Give chores a clear and sensible outcome

A reward can help when a child needs a little extra encouragement, but it should not make every small responsibility feel like a financial deal. The most useful systems stay light, predictable and easy to explain.

You can decide which chores earn progress and which are simply part of family life. Once a task is marked complete, you review it before any reward coins are added.

The reward itself stays a family decision too. Parents can choose something modest, free or experience-based if that suits the household better, and they can change the idea without turning every chore into a promise that the app itself is responsible for fulfilling.

The parent keeps the final say. Tasks do not add approved progress until the parent has reviewed them using the protected parent controls.

Quick questions

Who is responsible for the rewards connected to chores?

The parent or guardian creates the rewards and provides them when they decide the child has reached the agreed goal.

Does Vanta Labs fund or deliver chore rewards?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee rewards created by a family for completed chores.

Can a family change which rewards are linked to chores?

Yes. Parents can revise the reward choices as routines, interests and what works at home change.
