Let children play a useful part at home

Putting toys away, setting the table or helping with a pet can become familiar parts of family life. Parent Quest keeps those responsibilities visible.

The parent chooses the chores and can change them as the child grows. The aim is participation and confidence, not an endless list of jobs.

Parents can also vary how often those chores appear. Putting toys away might be part of the daily routine, while another household job may only make sense once a week, helping the list stay realistic rather than unnecessarily repetitive.

Home chores should remain age appropriate. Parents decide which jobs are safe and whether the child needs help or supervision.

Quick questions

Do home chores for kids all need to be daily?

No. Parents choose which home chores should repeat every day and which should stay occasional.

Can each child have different home chores?

Yes. Chores can be organised separately around each child profile and the responsibilities the parent considers appropriate.

Who decides whether a child has completed a home chore?

The parent or guardian reviews the child’s submitted progress and decides whether the chore should be approved.
