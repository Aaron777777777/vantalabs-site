What the organisation should expect from the process

Businesses normally consider Android and iOS workforce app because the current process is slow to check, difficult to consolidate or too dependent on memory. A clear process tells the worker to use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation. The relevant evidence includes workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for Android and iOS workforce app?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for Android and iOS workforce app?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
