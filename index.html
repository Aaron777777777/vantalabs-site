Cigar Prices UK

Find the cheapest cigar prices in the UK.

Compare UK retailers vs EU imports and see where you actually save per cigar and per box.

Cigar prices vary massively between retailers.

The same cigar can cost far more at one UK shop than another — and sometimes less again when importing from Europe.

Most smokers either overpay or spend ages checking multiple sites before buying.

Cigar Ledger does the checking for you.

  • Compares UK cigar retailers
  • Calculates EU landed import cost
  • Shows savings per cigar and per box
  • Highlights the best current buying route
Example saving

UK price: £52 · EU landed: £38

That is £14 saved per cigar — and £350+ saved on a 25-count box.
