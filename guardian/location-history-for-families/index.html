Recent history for understanding phone-location context

Recent location history can provide useful context when a parent needs to understand what a connected child phone has reported, particularly when a routine changes or the latest map position needs more explanation. Guardian keeps that history alongside the private Family Map, Home and School alerts, battery status, last-update context and SOS within the parent-managed family experience.

Location history should not be treated as a perfectly complete movement record. It depends on the location information the phone was able to provide, so gaps, delays or inaccuracies can occur because of GPS, connectivity, buildings, permissions, battery conditions or operating-system restrictions. Used proportionately, recent history can add context without replacing direct family communication or proving somebody’s exact whereabouts.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What is Guardian location history useful for?

It can add context by showing recent location information reported by a connected child phone when a parent needs to understand a journey or changing routine.

Is Guardian location history a complete record of every movement?

No. It depends on the location updates available from the phone, so gaps, delays or inaccuracies can occur.

Can recent location history prove exactly where somebody was?

No. It is useful phone-location context rather than a legally authoritative or perfectly precise record of physical presence.
