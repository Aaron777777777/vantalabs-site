Place-based context for familiar family routines

Family place alerts are most useful when they relate to locations that genuinely matter to a routine. Guardian centres its safe-zone experience on Home and School, allowing trusted adults to receive arrival and leaving context from a connected child phone without treating every movement as something that needs a notification.

A place alert relies on the phone’s available location information and the configured area around the familiar place. It is not an exact attendance record and cannot prove that somebody is physically at one precise point. GPS, signal, permissions, connectivity, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect reporting, so alerts are best used alongside ordinary family communication.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What are family place alerts useful for?

They can provide useful arrival and leaving context around familiar routines such as Home and School without requiring constant map checking.

Does Guardian create an alert for every movement?

No. Guardian focuses on configured Home and School areas rather than notifying trusted adults about every movement of the phone.

Can a place alert be treated as proof of attendance?

No. It is phone-location context and can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, buildings, battery conditions and other device factors.
