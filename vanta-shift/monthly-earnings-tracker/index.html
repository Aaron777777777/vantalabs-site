Quick questions

Can I review estimated earnings for a whole month?

Yes. Vanta Shift can use the shifts and pay details you enter to show a personal monthly earnings estimate for planning.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. Start and finish times can cross midnight so overnight work remains clear in the calendar.

Does monthly earnings tracker manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
