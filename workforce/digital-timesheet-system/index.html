A clearer record for the real working day

Businesses normally look for digital timesheet system when the working day is creating information in too many separate places. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is being able to review available hours and identify missing attendance.

Authorised managers can use the available record to examine whether the recorded periods accurately describe the hours under review. That evidence includes My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records and should be read with its timing and workplace circumstances. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review incomplete attendance being mistaken for a final payroll-ready total proportionately.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for digital timesheet system?

The relevant view can include My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
