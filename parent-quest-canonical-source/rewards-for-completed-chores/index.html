Rewards for Completed Chores

Recognise completed chores with progress towards a reward, but only after you have checked the work yourself.

Google Play App Store

Link rewards to chores you have reviewed

A tick on a screen should not be the final word. Children can mark a chore as finished, then the parent can look at what was done before deciding whether it counts.

That extra check keeps expectations clear and avoids arguments about unfinished jobs earning the same progress as properly completed ones. Approved chores can then contribute to the child’s reward goal.

A completed chore still needs approval. The parent reviews submitted chores before confirming any associated reward progress.

Quick questions

Who creates the rewards?

The parent or guardian creates and provides every reward.

Are rewards supplied by Vanta Labs?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee family rewards.

Can rewards be changed?

Yes. Parents can change the reward list as the family’s interests and circumstances change.
