Family Child Profiles

Organise several children inside one family setup while keeping each child’s tasks, progress and rewards distinct.

Google Play App Store

Keep the family together without mixing progress

A shared household routine should not mean combining everybody’s work into one total. Each child needs to see what belongs to them and what they have already completed.

Family child profiles keep those records separate while giving the parent one place to manage the overall setup. That makes it easier to stay fair and spot what each child needs next.

Each profile keeps its own records. Tasks, approved progress and rewards for one child are not added to another child’s profile.

Quick questions

Can each child have their own profile?

Yes. Each child can have separate chores, progress and rewards.

Do child profiles need separate accounts?

No. They remain part of the parent-managed family setup.

How much personal information is needed?

Parents should only enter what the family needs. A first name or nickname is often enough.
