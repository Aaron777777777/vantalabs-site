Workforce Management App

Manage worker attendance, workplaces, working hours and operational status in one mobile workforce system for managers and staff.

Google Play App Store

Make the process easier to follow and review

Workforce Management App works best when the organisation starts with the real daily routine and uses the app to make that routine easier to record. For the worker, the expected action is to use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation, by checking workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status against the organisation’s actual setup. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations. In the context of Workforce Management App, the organisation should also record who is responsible for resolving any exception.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce management?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for workforce management?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
