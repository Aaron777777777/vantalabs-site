Quick questions

Can an overnight shift cross into the following day?

Yes. A shift can start on one date and finish after midnight, allowing overnight work to remain clear in your personal calendar.

Can I use this with rotating work?

Yes. The calendar supports repeating day, night and off patterns as well as individually entered shifts.

Does overnight shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
