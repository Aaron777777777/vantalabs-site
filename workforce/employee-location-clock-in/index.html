A clearer record for the real working day

The question behind employee location clock-in is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? From the worker’s perspective, success means knowing when location is requested and what happens when it is unavailable. A fair exception route should also exist for genuine mistakes and device problems.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess whether the location context supports the recorded workplace activity, using attendance information supported by the latest available device-location context and the surrounding workplace context. Rollout should include a short explanation, a test action and a known support route, particularly because of poor signal, restricted permissions, GPS drift or delayed location updates.

What to keep in mind Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.
