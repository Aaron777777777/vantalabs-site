Quick questions

What is the benefit of keeping my work rota digitally?

A digital personal rota keeps upcoming shifts, times and days off together so you can check the schedule you have entered without searching through old messages.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does digital work rota manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
