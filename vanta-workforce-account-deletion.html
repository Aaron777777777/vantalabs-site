Vanta Workforce Account Deletion

Request account deletion

If you would like to delete your Vanta Workforce account and associated personal data, you can request deletion by contacting Vanta Labs.

Please email us from the email address associated with your Vanta Workforce account:

Email: support@vantalabs.com

What happens next?

We will verify ownership of the account and process your deletion request. Once verified, your account and associated personal data will be deleted.

Data retention

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or business record purposes.

Need help?

If you have any questions about your data or account, please contact Vanta Labs using the email address above.
