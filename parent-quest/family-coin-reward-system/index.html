Family Coin Reward System

Create one family coin system with separate progress for each child and rewards chosen by the parent.

Use shared rules without mixing everyone together

Families may want the same basic approach for all children while still giving each child their own tasks, balances and goals. That keeps the routine consistent without losing track of individual progress.

Parents control the coin values and reward choices from the same device. Each child’s profile keeps their approved progress separate, even when siblings are working towards similar rewards.

A shared family approach can therefore use consistent rules without combining everybody’s balance. Parents can keep each child’s progress separate and still decide which chores, coin values and family rewards make sense across the household.

Each child keeps their own progress. Coins recorded for one child are not shared with or added to another child’s balance.

Quick questions

Are the coins in a family reward system actual currency?

No. Family reward coins are only an in-app progress measure and carry no cash value.

Who sets the rules for a family coin reward system?

The parent sets the chores, coin values, approval rules and family rewards used in the system.

Can family reward coins be purchased or redeemed through Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be bought from Vanta Labs, withdrawn for money or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
