2. Information we may collect

The information we collect depends on which app or service is used, which features are enabled and which device permissions are granted.

Account information

Where an app requires or supports an account, we may process:

  • email address;
  • display name;
  • sign-in provider;
  • account identifiers;
  • family or group identifiers; and
  • authentication information required to sign the user in securely.

Where a user signs in through Apple, Google or another authentication provider, we receive only the information made available by that provider in accordance with the user’s settings and authorisation.

Guardian location information

When Guardian location-sharing or safety features are enabled, Guardian may process:

  • precise device location;
  • background location where permission has been granted;
  • location accuracy and timestamps;
  • location history;
  • safe-zone locations;
  • safe-zone arrival and departure events; and
  • family-group information needed to display location information to connected family members.

Location permissions can be managed through the device settings. Disabling precise location, background location or related permissions may prevent Guardian features from working correctly.

Guardian SOS and safety information

Guardian may process:

  • SOS and safety-alert events;
  • event timestamps and status;
  • device battery information;
  • relevant device status;
  • connected family-member information;
  • push-notification tokens; and
  • notification information needed to send alerts.

Herf Station information

Herf Station may process:

  • account information;
  • profile information;
  • lounge information;
  • lounge participant information;
  • reports or moderation information where applicable;
  • push-notification information; and

We may process limited technical information required to operate, secure and improve our apps, including:

5. Guardian location and safety features

Guardian is designed to help authorised family members share device locations and receive safety-related notifications.

Who can see Guardian information

Guardian location and safety information may be visible to authorised members of the relevant connected family group.

The family organiser or account holder is responsible for deciding who is invited, inviting only people they trust, protecting access to the account and removing members when access is no longer appropriate.

Vanta Labs does not independently verify every claimed family relationship or every claim of parental responsibility.

Location accuracy and notification limitations

Guardian relies on devices, networks, operating systems and third-party services outside our complete control.

Location information, location history, safe-zone events, push notifications and SOS alerts may be delayed, inaccurate, incomplete, duplicated or unavailable because of internet or mobile-network availability, GPS accuracy, device hardware, battery level, battery-saving settings, disabled permissions, notification settings, operating-system restrictions, app termination, device shutdown, incorrect safe-zone settings or third-party service interruptions.

Guardian is not an emergency service

Guardian is not an emergency service, alarm-monitoring service, medical device, professional safeguarding service or guaranteed location-tracking service.

Guardian must not be relied upon as the sole means of protecting or locating a person or obtaining emergency assistance.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services directly.

6. Children’s privacy

Guardian and Parent Quest include features intended to be managed by parents or guardians.

If an adult creates or manages a child profile, connects a child device, adds information relating to a child, enables Guardian location sharing for a child or allows another person to view a child’s location, that adult confirms that they are the child’s parent or legal guardian, or otherwise have appropriate authority.

Parents and guardians are responsible for using the apps lawfully, acting in the child’s best interests, explaining location sharing or monitoring where appropriate, ensuring device permissions are appropriate, controlling who is connected and removing access when it is no longer appropriate.

Guardian must not be used for stalking, harassment, domestic abuse, coercive or controlling behaviour, covert monitoring without appropriate authority, unauthorised workplace monitoring or any other unlawful surveillance.

Where a privacy request concerns a child, we may request reasonable evidence of identity, parental responsibility or other authority.
