What businesses should decide first

The question behind how overnight shifts affect timesheets is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? The worker-facing process centres on being able to review available hours and identify missing attendance. It should remain understandable during busy arrivals, departures, site moves and scheduled checks.

The manager view is intended to make whether the recorded periods accurately describe the hours under review easier to review. It presents My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review incomplete attendance being mistaken for a final payroll-ready total proportionately.
