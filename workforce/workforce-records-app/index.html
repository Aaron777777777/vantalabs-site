Workforce Records App

Keep workplace, worker-role, attendance and operational records together so authorised managers can review workforce activity in one place.

Google Play App Store

Replace scattered updates with one consistent record

Workforce Records App is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should use only the attendance and check actions appropriate to their role.

The management side is about being able to keep workplaces, access and worker records aligned with the real organisation. That judgement is supported by workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used. The practical test for Workforce Records App is whether someone unfamiliar with the original event can still understand the record later.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce records?

The available record can include workplaces, user roles, attendance activity and operational status, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for workforce records?

The organisation remains responsible for access, configuration, workplace policy and ongoing administration.
