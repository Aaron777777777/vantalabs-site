Let workers record when their working period begins and give authorised managers a clear starting point for attendance and hours review.

Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to worker booking on begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to complete a clear booking-on or booking-off action at the right time, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to identify missing endings, unusual durations and records needing correction. The relevant evidence includes the beginning and end of each recorded attendance period. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction. That distinction is particularly important when Worker Booking-On App is being used across more than one worker or workplace.

Clocking in and out still needs sensible review. A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for worker booking on?

The available record can include the beginning and end of each recorded attendance period, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for worker booking on?

A digital timestamp supports review, but it cannot explain every workplace circumstance by itself.
