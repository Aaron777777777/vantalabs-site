Give children personal chore lists without asking them to create accounts, remember passwords or use their own phones.

Keep child profiles inside the family account

A child profile is enough to separate tasks and rewards without turning every family member into an independent app user. Parents can use a first name or nickname to keep profiles recognisable.

Everything remains managed through the parent’s device. Children can view their routine and submit completed tasks without needing an email address or personal login.

This keeps child profiles focused on organisation rather than account management. Parents can separate chores and progress for siblings without creating passwords, recovery details or another set of credentials for children to remember.

Profiles are not standalone accounts. Children do not register separately or manage their own Parent Quest account.

Quick questions

Do children need their own device for a Parent Quest profile?

No. The family routine is managed from the parent-owned device rather than requiring a phone for each child.

Is a child profile the same as creating a child account?

No. A child profile organises that child’s chores and progress inside the parent-managed family setup without creating a separate email-based account.

Who manages the protected parts of the family setup?

The parent or guardian manages protected settings, approval and reward decisions.
