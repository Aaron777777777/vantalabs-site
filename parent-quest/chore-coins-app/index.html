Chore Coins App

Attach simple coin values to selected chores so children can see how approved work contributes to a larger goal.

Google Play App Store

Let completed chores add up clearly

Some rewards are too large to connect sensibly to one small job. Reward coins allow several completed chores to build towards the goal over time instead.

Parents choose the coin value, check the finished chore and approve the progress. That means the balance reflects work the parent has actually reviewed rather than every task a child marks complete.

Coins follow parent approval. A chore must be reviewed before its confirmed coin value is added to the child’s progress.

Quick questions

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are an in-app way to show progress and have no cash value.

Who decides how many coins a task is worth?

The parent controls how reward coins are used inside the family routine.

Can coins be exchanged with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be withdrawn, purchased from or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
