Worker Safety Check App

Support regular worker safety checks with scheduled prompts, phone-check activity and manager visibility when an expected response is missing.

Google Play App Store

Replace scattered updates with one consistent record

Worker Safety Check App is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure.

The management side is about being able to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. That judgement is supported by scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used. For Worker Safety Check App, the clearest outcome is a record that can be explained without relying on memory or informal messages.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for worker safety checks?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for worker safety checks?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
