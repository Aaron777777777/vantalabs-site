Reading Reward Chart

Reading rewards can be kept simple: define the task, let the child mark it complete and review it before approving progress.

Parents choose the reward structure and can create custom rewards that fit their household. Parent Quest reward coins have no cash value.

Because the rewards come from the family rather than an external catalogue, parents can keep them age-appropriate and realistic. They can also explain exactly what a reward means before a child starts working towards it, then change future choices when needed.

You choose and provide every reward. Parent Quest keeps the list organised, but the rewards themselves always come from the parent or guardian.

Quick questions

What is reading reward chart?

Does Parent Quest require a separate child account?

No. Parent Quest is designed around parent-managed child profiles rather than separate child accounts.

Are Parent Quest reward coins real money?

No. Reward coins are an internal family reward system and have no cash value.
