A private family locator app with no public profiles

Guardian is designed as a private family locator rather than a public location network. Connected child-device information stays within the parent-managed family for the trusted adults who have been given access.

Family location sharing with clearer boundaries

Those adults can use the Family Map, device status and place-based alerts to understand everyday journeys without publishing family location information through public profiles or social discovery.

Parents keep control over whether their own location is shared. Adult location sharing remains optional, and family access should be reviewed whenever somebody no longer needs to see the child-device information.

A private family locator still depends on the connected phone for location reporting, so GPS, permissions, connectivity and battery conditions can affect accuracy and timing. Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Who can access location information in a private Guardian family?

The available connected-device information is for trusted adults who have access to that parent-managed family.

Can a parent keep their own location private while using child-device features?

Yes. Adult location sharing is optional and does not have to be enabled to use authorised child-device information.

What should happen when a caregiver no longer needs Guardian access?

The family should review its trusted adults and remove access that is no longer needed when caregiving or family circumstances change.
