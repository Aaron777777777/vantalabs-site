Use a School arrival alert instead of repeated messages

The School safe zone should represent the place as a practical area rather than a single point. Entrances, playgrounds and larger school grounds can all sit within the everyday location variation of a phone.

Quick questions

Can Guardian reduce the need to ask whether my child has reached School?

A School arrival alert can provide a useful routine milestone when the connected child phone appears to enter the configured School area.

Why should a School safe zone cover an area rather than one precise point?

Phone GPS naturally varies around large buildings, entrances and school grounds, so a practical area is more useful than expecting one exact coordinate.

Does a School notification prove my child is inside the classroom?

No. It reflects the connected phone entering the configured School safe zone and is not a formal attendance check.
