Kids Chore Profiles

Keep each child’s chore list separate so they can focus on their own jobs instead of sorting through everybody else’s.

Google Play App Store

Give every child a clearer place to start

A mixed family list can quickly become confusing, especially when siblings have different ages and responsibilities. Individual profiles make it obvious which tasks belong to whom.

Parents still manage everything from the same device. The profiles organise the routine without creating separate logins or handing account control to children.

That makes the child-facing experience easier to understand too. Each child can focus on the chores assigned to their profile instead of sorting through a family-wide list, while the adult continues to control changes, approval and rewards.

Profiles are not logins for children. They are sections inside the parent-managed family setup on the parent’s device.

Quick questions

Can kids have different chores in separate profiles?

Yes. A separate profile can show each child the chores and progress that belong to them rather than combining every sibling’s responsibilities.

Do kids need usernames or passwords for their chore profiles?

No. The profiles remain inside the parent-managed setup, so children do not need separate account credentials.

How much information is required to identify each child?

Parents can use only what they need to distinguish the profiles. A first name or family nickname can be enough.
