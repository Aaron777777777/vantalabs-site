Quick questions

How do shift notifications help with a rota?

Notifications can help you remember upcoming shifts and unusual start times from the rota you have saved in your personal calendar.

Can I use this with rotating work?

Yes. The calendar supports repeating day, night and off patterns as well as individually entered shifts.

Does how shift notifications work manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
