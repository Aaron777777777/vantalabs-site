Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to attendance reporting begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to keep booking activity complete so the later report reflects the working period, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to select the correct workers and dates, then review exceptions before export. The relevant evidence includes attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Attendance reporting and exports still needs sensible review. Reports and exports are operational records and require verification before further use.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for attendance reporting?

The available record can include attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for attendance reporting?

Reports are operational records and should be verified before payroll, accounting, legal or employment decisions.
