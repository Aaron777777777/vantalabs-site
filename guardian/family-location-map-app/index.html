Each connected child phone can be shown with its latest available shared location, battery level and last-update information. Those details make it easier to understand whether a map position appears current.

Home and School alerts handle many predictable journey milestones, which means the Family Map can stay focused on the occasions when a parent actually needs more detail.

Quick questions

What does the Guardian Family Map show for a connected child phone?

It can show the latest available shared location together with useful context such as battery level and last-update time.

Who can access the Guardian Family Map?

The map is available to trusted adults with access to the private Guardian family rather than being exposed through a public social map.

Does every adult in Guardian have to share their own location?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and can be enabled or left off separately from child-device location features.
