Quick questions

Can siblings have different jobs in the same family task app?

Yes. Separate child profiles allow parents to give different tasks to different children while keeping the wider family routine together in one app.

Who manages the family task lists?

The parent or guardian manages the lists from the parent-owned device and decides which jobs belong to each child.

Does the app choose suitable family jobs automatically?

No. Parent Quest organises the jobs entered by the parent; the adult still decides what is safe, realistic and appropriate for each child.
