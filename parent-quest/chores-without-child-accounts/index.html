Give children personal chore lists without asking them to create accounts, remember passwords or use their own phones.

Keep child profiles inside the family account

A child profile is enough to separate tasks and rewards without turning every family member into an independent app user. Parents can use a first name or nickname to keep profiles recognisable.

Everything remains managed through the parent’s device. Children can view their routine and submit completed tasks without needing an email address or personal login.

Profiles are not standalone accounts. Children do not register separately or manage their own Parent Quest account.

Quick questions

Does every child need their own phone?

No. Parent Quest is designed to be managed from a parent-owned device.

Do children need email accounts?

No. Child profiles are not separate email-based accounts.

Who controls settings and approval?

The parent or guardian controls protected settings, approval and rewards.
