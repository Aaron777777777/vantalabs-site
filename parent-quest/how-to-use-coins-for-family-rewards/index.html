How to Use Coins for Family Rewards

Use reward coins as a simple progress marker towards family-created rewards rather than treating them like real money.

Google Play App Store

Choose values children can understand

Keep the numbers straightforward. A child should be able to see how regular approved tasks move them towards the reward without needing complicated calculations.

Parents decide which chores earn coins, how many are added and what each reward requires. The coins remain entirely inside the family routine and have no cash value.

Reward coins are not currency. They cannot be purchased, withdrawn or exchanged with Vanta Labs.

Quick questions

Should every family use the same chore routine?

No. Chores and rewards should suit the individual child and household.

How many chores should parents start with?

A small, realistic list is usually easier to explain and maintain.

Can the routine be changed later?

Yes. Parents should review what works and adjust anything that creates unnecessary friction.
