Quick questions

How can reminders help me avoid overlooking a work shift?

A reminder tied to the shifts you have saved can give you an extra prompt before upcoming work, especially when the date or start time is unusual.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does never miss a work shift manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
