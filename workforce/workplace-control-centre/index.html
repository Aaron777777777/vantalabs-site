Make the process easier to follow and review

Workplace Control Centre works best when the organisation starts with the real daily routine and uses the app to make that routine easier to record. For the worker, the expected action is to complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it, by considering worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records together with the surrounding workplace context. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations. That distinction is particularly important when Workplace Control Centre is being used across more than one worker or workplace.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workplace control centre?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for workplace control centre?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
