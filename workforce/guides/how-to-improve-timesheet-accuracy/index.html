The practical thinking behind how to improve timesheet accuracy

How to Improve Timesheet Accuracy is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should review recorded hours and flag anything that appears incomplete.

Authorised managers can then use booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods to check the underlying attendance periods before using a monthly total. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Timesheets and working hours still needs sensible review. Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

What does Vanta Workforce record for how to improve timesheet accuracy?
