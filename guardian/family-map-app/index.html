family map app

One private family map for the people who matter

A family map is most valuable when there is a reason to open it. Guardian gives trusted adults a private place to check a connected child phone after an unexpected delay, changed plan or alert that needs more context.

Google PlayApp Store

Use the map when an alert needs more context

The latest available shared location appears alongside battery and last-update information, helping parents judge whether the device seems to be reporting normally.

For predictable journeys, Home and School alerts can provide routine milestones without requiring somebody to keep the map open. The map then remains available for the moments that actually need a closer look.

Location can still drift or update late because of GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions or battery restrictions. Guardian cannot guarantee notification delivery or exact positioning and is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

When is the Guardian Family Map most useful?

It is useful when an alert, delay or changed plan means a trusted adult wants more context about a connected child phone.

How can I judge whether a Family Map position is current?

Battery status and last-update information provide useful context alongside the latest available location.

Should the Family Map be relied on during an emergency?

No. Guardian is not an emergency service. Immediate danger should be handled through direct contact and the emergency services.
