Quick questions

What makes Vanta Shift a personal rota calendar?

You enter and maintain your own shifts, times, notes and rota pattern on the device; it does not manage other workers or an employer schedule.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does personal rota calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
