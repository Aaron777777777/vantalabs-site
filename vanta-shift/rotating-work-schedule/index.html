Quick questions

What is rotating work schedule useful for?

It helps you place a complete working cycle across the calendar quickly using the repeating shift pattern stored on your device.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does rotating work schedule manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
