Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

The practical purpose of workforce control centre is not simply to digitise a form. It is to produce a clearer record that remains useful after the working day has finished. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is having relevant attendance and check actions reflected accurately.

The manager view is intended to make which workers are active and which records require attention easier to review. It presents worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents making decisions from stale, incomplete or misunderstood device data from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

What to keep in mind Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce control centre?

The relevant view can include worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.
