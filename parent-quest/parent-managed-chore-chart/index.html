The parent keeps the final say

Children can take part in the routine without having access to the settings that control it. The parent creates the chores, chooses the rewards and reviews completed work.

Protected actions stay behind the Parent PIN, so the family can share one device without handing over control of the system.

This separation lets children take part without turning the shared device into an unrestricted settings screen. They can see their chores and record progress, while changes to the setup, approval and reward decisions remain protected for the parent.

Keep the Parent PIN to yourself. The PIN protects approval, rewards and other actions that should remain with the parent or guardian.

Quick questions

Who can change a parent-managed chore chart?

The parent controls the chart and protected settings, including which chores belong to each child and when those chores are changed.

Can children approve their own completed chores?

No. Children can mark progress, but protected parent approval remains separate before completion or reward progress is confirmed.

Does the protected chore chart replace parental supervision?

No. The Parent PIN protects app controls; the parent or guardian still remains responsible for the child and any real-world supervision.
