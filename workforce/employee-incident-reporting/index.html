A clearer record for the real working day

Employee incident reporting is most useful when a business wants to replace scattered messages, paper records or end-of-month guesswork with one repeatable process. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is knowing what response is expected and who follows up when something is wrong.

The manager view is intended to make which checks are due, complete or require a human response easier to review. It presents scheduled prompts, worker phone checks and relevant incident information without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. Good records begin before anyone opens the app. The organisation should set clear expectations and establish how treating an app notification as a complete workplace-safety system will be handled.
