Connect the worker action with the later review

When live workforce status is handled consistently, the resulting record is easier for both workers and authorised managers to understand. For the worker, the expected action is to complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it, by reviewing worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records alongside the circumstances around each record. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for live workforce status?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for live workforce status?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
