A simpler way to review the working day

Multi-site attendance can bring structure to a process that otherwise depends heavily on memory, messages or manual reconciliation. Workers are more likely to use the process consistently when they understand its purpose. In this case, that purpose is knowing that the expected booking action has been received.

Authorised managers can use the available record to examine who is currently recorded as working at the relevant workplace. That evidence includes the latest available worker status connected to attendance history and should be read with its timing and workplace circumstances. Rollout should include a short explanation, a test action and a known support route, particularly because of confusing delayed device information with a definite absence.

Workplace presence still needs sensible review. Current status is an operational view and may be affected by delayed or incomplete data.

What does Vanta Workforce record for multi-site attendance?

The relevant view can include the latest available worker status connected to attendance history, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
