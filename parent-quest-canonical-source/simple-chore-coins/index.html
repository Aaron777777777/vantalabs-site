Use only the amount of structure you need

There is no benefit in creating a complicated economy for a household chore list. A few understandable values can be enough to show that regular effort is moving a child towards a goal.

The system can stay small: selected chores, simple coin amounts and a short reward list. Parents can change it whenever the routine stops feeling helpful.

Coins are just a progress marker. They cannot be purchased, cashed out or exchanged for money through Parent Quest or Vanta Labs.

Quick questions

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are an in-app way to show progress and have no cash value.

Who decides how many coins a task is worth?

The parent controls how reward coins are used inside the family routine.

Can coins be exchanged with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be withdrawn, purchased from or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
