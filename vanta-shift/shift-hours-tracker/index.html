Quick questions

Can I track the hours attached to my shifts?

Yes. The start and finish times you enter can be used to review the scheduled hours connected to your personal rota.

Does this require a Vanta Shift account?

No. Vanta Shift is designed as a personal planner without an account or workplace setup.

Does shift hours tracker manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
