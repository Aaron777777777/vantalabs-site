Quick questions

How can Guardian support an ordinary school run?

Home and School alerts can provide useful journey milestones while the Family Map remains available when a parent needs additional context.

Does using Guardian make a school journey guaranteed safe?

No. Guardian provides family location and alert tools but cannot guarantee safety, and ordinary communication and planning remain important.

What can affect school-run location updates?

GPS conditions, connectivity, location permissions, background restrictions and battery behaviour can all affect reporting from the child phone.
