Extra context when a routine changes

Sometimes a parent simply needs extra context because a pickup changes, somebody is later than expected or a familiar routine does not go to plan. Guardian gives trusted adults a private Family Map for that occasional check, alongside battery and last-update information that can help explain what the connected child phone has most recently reported.

For ordinary journeys, Home and School alerts can handle many routine updates so parents do not need to keep opening the map. Recent location context can also help make sense of what the phone has reported when circumstances change. Guardian does not promise exact or continuous location: GPS, connectivity, permissions, buildings, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect updates. The app is intended to provide useful family context while leaving room for trust, independence and direct communication.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

When is a quick Guardian location check useful?

It can be useful when a pickup changes, somebody is later than expected or a parent needs extra context about what the connected child phone last reported.

Can Home and School alerts reduce manual map checks?

Yes. Place alerts can handle many routine arrival and leaving updates so manual map checks can be reserved for times when more context is useful.

Who can access family location information in Guardian?

Access is limited to trusted adults within the private Guardian family rather than public or social profiles.
