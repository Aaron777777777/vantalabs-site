Combine simple reward coins with rewards created by your family, so progress leads towards something the child actually values.

Connect visible progress to personal rewards

Coins are most useful when the goal at the end means something to the child. Parents can create that goal themselves instead of relying on a fixed catalogue that may not suit the family.

As approved chores and tasks add to the balance, the child can see the gap closing. The parent still decides when progress counts and how the chosen reward is provided.

Coins and rewards stay inside the family. Vanta Labs does not sell reward coins, redeem them or provide the custom rewards listed by the parent.

Quick questions

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are an in-app way to show progress and have no cash value.

Who decides how many coins a task is worth?

The parent controls how reward coins are used inside the family routine.

Can coins be exchanged with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be withdrawn, purchased from or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
