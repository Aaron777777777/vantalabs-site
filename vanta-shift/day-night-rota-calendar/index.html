Quick questions

How can I organise a rota that switches between days and nights?

Add day shifts, night shifts and rest days to the same personal calendar so you can see the full rotation and adjust individual dates when your real rota changes.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does day and night rota calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
