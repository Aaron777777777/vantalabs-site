Parent Checks for Chores

Add a simple parent check before completed chores contribute to progress, coins or family rewards.

Google Play App Store

Keep rewards tied to chores you have seen

A parent check helps children understand that finishing well matters more than pressing a button. It also gives you space to recognise effort when a task was difficult or needs another attempt.

The aim is not to turn every chore into an inspection. It is to give the parent a reliable final say before the app treats the task as approved.

Some checks may take only a few seconds, while others may involve asking the child to finish one small part of the job. The important point is that progress is not confirmed automatically before the parent has had the chance to make that decision.

Use your judgement, not rigid automation. Parent Quest supports the parent’s decision instead of trying to decide whether a chore was done properly.

Quick questions

Why are parent chore checks protected by a PIN?

The PIN helps keep the review and approval step on the adult side of the app when the family shares one device.

Can children perform the final parent check themselves?

No. The child can submit completed work, but the parent or guardian performs the final review.

Does every parent check need to be detailed?

No. A check can be as simple as confirming the job was done before the app records it as approved progress.
