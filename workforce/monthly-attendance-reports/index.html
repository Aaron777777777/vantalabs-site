Replace scattered updates with one consistent record

Monthly Attendance Reports is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should keep booking activity complete so the later report reflects the working period.

The management side is about being able to select the correct workers and dates, then review exceptions before export. That judgement is supported by attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Attendance reporting and exports still needs sensible review. Reports and exports are operational records and require verification before further use.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for monthly attendance reports?

The available record can include attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for monthly attendance reports?

Reports are operational records and should be verified before payroll, accounting, legal or employment decisions.
