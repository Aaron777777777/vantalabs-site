Tasks and Rewards for Kids

Connect completed tasks with rewards chosen by your family, without turning every small job into a negotiation.

Google Play App Store

Let progress build towards something meaningful

Rewards can help children see that consistent effort adds up. Parent Quest lets parents create rewards that fit their own family rather than choosing from a fixed catalogue.

Reward coins show progress inside the app, while the parent decides when tasks count and which rewards are available.

Families can therefore separate the job from the reward decision. Some tasks may earn progress towards a chosen reward while others can simply remain everyday responsibilities. The parent controls both the task list and the reward setup rather than relying on an automatic catalogue.

Reward coins are not money. They have no cash value and are only used inside the family’s own reward routine.

Quick questions

Can different children have different tasks and rewards?

Yes. Child profiles can have separate tasks and progress, while parents choose which rewards make sense for each child and family routine.

Who creates the tasks and chooses the rewards?

The parent or guardian manages both. Parent Quest does not supply the jobs or the family rewards.

Does Parent Quest decide which tasks deserve a reward?

No. The parent decides which tasks are appropriate, whether a task should contribute towards reward progress and when completion should be approved.
