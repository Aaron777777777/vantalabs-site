How to use the information sensibly

The value of how to plan around night shifts comes from turning practical ways to understand assigned shifts, notifications, hours and estimated pay into a clearer personal calendar. The app provides a simpler way to make a changing work schedule easier to understand and plan around, while keeping official rota and payroll responsibilities with the employer.

It supports personal planning rather than replacing formal employer records.

Vanta Shift guide remains a personal planning view. The guidance is general and does not replace an employer’s rota, payroll information or workplace policy.
