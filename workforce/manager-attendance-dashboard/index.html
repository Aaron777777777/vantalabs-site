Replace scattered updates with one consistent record

Manager Attendance Dashboard is most useful when it fits naturally into the working day instead of becoming a separate administrative task. Workers need to know when to act, what the app records and how a genuine mistake can be corrected. In this case, that means they should complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app.

The management side is about being able to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it. That judgement is supported by worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, not replaced by it. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for manager attendance dashboard?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for manager attendance dashboard?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
