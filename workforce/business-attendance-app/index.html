From worker action to useful review

Businesses normally look for business attendance when the working day is creating information in too many separate places. From the worker’s perspective, success means seeing only the actions and information relevant to their role. A fair exception route should also exist for genuine mistakes and device problems.

The manager view is intended to make whether sites, access and worker records match the real organisation easier to review. It presents workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. Rollout should include a short explanation, a test action and a known support route, particularly because of unclear ownership of setup, corrections or ongoing administration.

Workforce operations still needs sensible review. The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for business attendance?

The relevant view can include workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
