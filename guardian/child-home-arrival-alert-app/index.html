Child Home Arrival Alert App, built around useful family context

A Home arrival alert can answer one ordinary question without another message or phone call: did the journey finish? Guardian lets a family create a Home safe zone so trusted adults can receive an update when the connected child device genuinely arrives inside that familiar area.

The alert is intended as practical family context rather than exact proof of somebody's position. Location accuracy can vary around buildings and roads, while signal, permissions and battery behaviour can delay updates. If something does not look right, direct family contact remains more important than the app.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What is child home arrival alert app used for?

Receive a Home arrival update when a connected child phone reaches its configured Home safe zone.

Can phone settings affect Guardian location updates?

Yes. GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions can affect location reporting and notification timing.

Is Guardian an emergency service?

No. Guardian provides family location and alert context. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
