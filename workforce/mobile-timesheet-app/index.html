Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

The practical purpose of mobile timesheets is not simply to digitise a form. It is to produce a clearer record that remains useful after the working day has finished. Workers mainly need being able to review available hours and identify missing attendance. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

The relevant management view focuses on whether the recorded periods accurately describe the hours under review. It uses My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records, while preserving the need to check unusual or incomplete records. The app works best inside an explicit operating process. Managers should know how to distinguish a genuine concern from incomplete attendance being mistaken for a final payroll-ready total.

What to keep in mind Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile timesheets?

The relevant view can include My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
