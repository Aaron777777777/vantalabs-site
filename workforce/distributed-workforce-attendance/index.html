Where the app helps—and where judgement still matters

Distributed Workforce Attendance can remove a great deal of end-of-day guesswork when everyone understands the action expected of them. The worker-facing part should remain simple: follow the same booking process while working across sites or field locations. A documented exception route is just as important as the normal flow.

Managers can use off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours to review distributed attendance without collecting separate messages from each site. Unusual records still need context before they are corrected or relied upon. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected.

Mobile and field workforces still needs sensible review. Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for distributed workforce attendance?

The available record can include off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can an unusual record be reviewed?

Yes. Authorised managers can examine the available timestamps and context, then follow the organisation’s correction process.

What limitation matters most for distributed workforce attendance?

The organisation still needs a fallback process for weak signal, device problems and unclear site information.
