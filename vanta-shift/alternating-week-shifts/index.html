Quick questions

What is alternating week shifts useful for?

It helps you generate months of working and rest days from one starting date using the common rotating rota stored on your device.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does alternating week shifts manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
