Quick questions

How can I keep a rotating day-and-night cycle organised?

Build the repeating sequence of day shifts, night shifts and rest days that you work, then use the correct starting date to keep the rotation aligned in your calendar.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does rotating day and night calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
