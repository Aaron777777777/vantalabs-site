Vanta Workforce Account Deletion

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Vanta Workforce helps organisations manage workforce attendance, shifts and workplace operations.

Request account deletion

If you would like your Vanta Workforce account and associated personal data removed, please contact your company administrator or manager.

Your company administrator manages access to your organisation's Vanta Workforce account and can process requests relating to your account and membership.

Important:
Please contact the organisation that manages your Vanta Workforce account rather than Vanta Labs directly.

What happens next?

Your company administrator will review your request, remove your account access where appropriate, and request deletion of applicable personal data.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or business record purposes.

Need help?

If you have questions about Vanta Workforce or need assistance with your account, please contact your company administrator first.
