The practical thinking behind how worker safety checks work

How Worker Safety Checks Work is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure.

Authorised managers can then use scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for how worker safety checks work?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.
