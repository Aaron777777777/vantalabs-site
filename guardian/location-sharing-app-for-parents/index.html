Location sharing for parents who want useful updates

Explore location sharing app for parents with Guardian: private family access, useful alerts, device context and honest location limits.

Google PlayApp Store — coming soon

Location sharing that remains optional for adults

Guardian allows trusted adults to take part in a private family without forcing them to continuously share their own location. Adult location sharing can be used when it suits the family and left off when it does not.

Connected child-device information remains available to authorised family adults according to the app’s access controls. This keeps the focus on practical coordination rather than compulsory adult tracking.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Is Guardian available on iPhone and Android?

Guardian is available on Android through Google Play. The iPhone version is awaiting App Store approval.

Can parent location sharing ever be inaccurate?

Yes. GPS, signal, buildings, permissions and battery settings can affect location and timing.

Is Guardian an emergency service?

No. In immediate danger, contact the emergency services directly.
