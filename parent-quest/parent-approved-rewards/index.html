Keep rewards connected to work you have reviewed rather than allowing every completed-task tap to count automatically.

Make approval part of the reward routine

Rewards feel fairer when children know the same rule applies each time: finish the task, let the parent check it and then receive the agreed progress.

That small approval step stops unfinished jobs from adding reward coins and gives parents room to use their judgement when effort, age or circumstances need to be considered.

The parent confirms reward progress. Children can submit tasks, but they cannot approve their own coins or rewards.

Quick questions

Why does Parent Quest use a Parent PIN?

The PIN helps keep approval and protected parent actions under adult control.

Can children approve their own chores?

No. Approval is intended to remain with the parent or guardian.

Does approval have to be complicated?

No. It is designed as a quick parent check before progress is confirmed.
