Child location

See a child’s latest shared location when it matters

Guardian connects a child phone to a parent-managed family so trusted adults can check its latest shared location, battery level and last-update time when a journey or routine needs context.

Google PlayApp Store

A calmer way to check a child’s latest location

The aim is not to encourage all-day map watching. Parents can use Home and School alerts for familiar milestones and open the Family Map when an unexpected delay, changed plan or missing update makes a closer check useful.

Phone location has practical limits. Buildings, weak signal, permissions, battery-saving behaviour and operating-system restrictions can all affect background reporting, so Guardian cannot promise perfect accuracy or uninterrupted tracking.

The connected child device also has access to Guardian’s SOS feature for contacting trusted adults. That adds another family communication route, but Guardian is not an emergency service and cannot replace direct emergency contact when someone may be in danger.

Quick questions

Does Guardian promise continuous child location tracking?

No. Guardian shows the latest available shared location, while phone settings, signal, permissions and battery behaviour can affect background updates.

How can I tell whether a child location update is recent?

Battery level and last-update time provide useful device context alongside the latest available location shown in Guardian.

What should I do if I believe the child is in immediate danger?

Contact the child and the emergency services directly. Guardian is a family location and alert tool, not an emergency service.
