Child Rewards Profile

Keep each child’s reward choices and approved progress alongside the tasks that belong to them.

Google Play App Store

Give rewards the right family context

Siblings may be working towards completely different things. One child might want to choose a day out while another is saving coins for extra game time.

A separate profile keeps those goals and balances clear. The parent can manage the reward list while the child sees progress connected to their own approved tasks.

Keeping reward progress separate also avoids one child’s balance being confused with a sibling’s. The parent can change available rewards when interests change while each child continues to see the progress connected to their own approved chores.

Rewards can differ between children. Parents choose what is suitable for each child rather than relying on one identical list.

Quick questions

Can each child have different reward progress?

Yes. Separate profiles keep each child’s approved progress and reward activity distinct, even when siblings are working towards different family rewards.

Does a child rewards profile need its own account?

No. The rewards profile belongs inside the parent-managed family setup rather than operating as an independent child account.

Do I need to enter lots of details for a rewards profile?

No. Parents can keep the profile minimal and use only enough information to identify the child within their own family setup.
