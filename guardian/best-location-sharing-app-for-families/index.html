How to choose a family location sharing app

Choosing a family location-sharing app starts with deciding who actually needs to see location information and why. Sharing boundaries can matter just as much as the map itself.

Quick questions

What privacy question should families ask before choosing a location-sharing app?

Ask who can access the location information, how family access is controlled and whether sharing choices can differ between children and adults.

Does Guardian require parents to share their own location?

No. Adult location sharing is optional even when a trusted adult can access authorised child-device information.

Can family location sharing replace normal check-ins completely?

No. Location tools can reduce routine messages, but phone reporting has normal limitations and direct family communication remains important.
