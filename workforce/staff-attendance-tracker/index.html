Making the workplace process easier to follow

Staff attendance tracking is most useful when a business wants to replace scattered messages, paper records or end-of-month guesswork with one repeatable process. For the worker, the important part is a clear booking action at the beginning and end of attendance. The organisation should explain what counts as completion and what to do when a device, account or connection problem prevents the normal action.

The manager view is intended to make whether the attendance record is complete and needs correction easier to review. It presents booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. The app works best inside an explicit operating process. Managers should know how to distinguish a genuine concern from forgotten bookings, delayed records or unusual attendance durations.

What to keep in mind Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for staff attendance tracking?

The relevant view can include booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.
