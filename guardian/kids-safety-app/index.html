Useful context without constant checking

Kids safety involves more than watching a dot move across a map. Guardian gives trusted adults useful context around everyday journeys through a private Family Map, Home and School alerts, battery and last-update information, recent location context and SOS. A parent can use those tools when a normal routine changes without needing to keep checking the phone throughout the day.

For familiar journeys, place alerts can reduce repeated check-in messages while the map remains available when more context is genuinely useful. Guardian shows the connected phone’s latest available location rather than promising an exact or continuous position. GPS, connectivity, buildings, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect updates and notification timing, so Guardian should support open family communication rather than replace it.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Does a kids safety app mean constantly watching a map?

No. Guardian is designed around useful alerts and occasional checks when extra family context is needed.

What information can help explain a child phone’s location?

Guardian can show the latest available phone location together with battery and last-update context, while Home and School alerts cover familiar places.

Can Guardian guarantee that every location alert arrives immediately?

No. GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can affect both location updates and notification timing.
