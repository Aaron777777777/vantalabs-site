Quick questions

What does a Guardian Home arrival alert mean?

It means the connected child phone has appeared to enter the configured Home safe zone based on the location information available to Guardian.

Why might a Home arrival notification appear later than expected?

GPS conditions, signal, permissions, background restrictions and battery behaviour can delay when the phone reports the boundary crossing.

What should a parent do if the Home alert is missing and they are concerned?

Use direct family contact rather than relying only on the app. Guardian provides location and alert context but is not an emergency service.
