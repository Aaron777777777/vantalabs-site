Keep several child routines manageable

Families with more than one child need separation without duplication. Each child can have their own tasks and goals while the parent moves between profiles from one family setup.

This keeps the routine practical on busy days. You can update one child’s list, review another child’s progress and still see how everything fits together.

Managing several profiles from one place also means changes can be made child by child. A parent might update one child’s morning chores, approve another child’s completed tasks and adjust a third child’s rewards without rebuilding the rest of the family routine.

One parent-managed setup is enough. Children do not need their own accounts, email addresses or passwords.

Quick questions

Can I manage several child profiles independently?

Yes. Parents can change chores, review progress and manage rewards for each child separately while keeping all profiles inside one family setup.

Does each child need a separate account or device?

No. Multiple child profiles are managed through the parent-owned setup without requiring individual child accounts or separate devices.

Do multiple profiles require detailed personal information?

No. Parents can keep each profile simple and enter only what helps them distinguish and manage the children in their own household.
