How can Vanta Shift help with an offshore rota?

Offshore workers can lay out longer work-and-rest rotations in one personal calendar and adjust individual dates when the expected cycle changes.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does offshore worker shift calendar manage other people?
