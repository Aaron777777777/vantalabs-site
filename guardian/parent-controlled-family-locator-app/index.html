A parent-controlled family locator for trusted adults

Guardian keeps family location features inside a parent-managed family rather than presenting child information through a public social profile. The family decides which trusted adults need access to the available child-device information.

A calmer way to stay informed

That parent-controlled approach is useful for everyday coordination because access can stay focused on the people who genuinely need it. Home and School alerts, map context and device information remain family tools rather than public-facing features.

Control also means reviewing the arrangement over time. Parents should reconsider trusted-adult access when family or caregiving circumstances change and explain to children how their information is being used.

Parent control does not make phone location perfectly accurate or guarantee notification delivery. GPS, connectivity, permissions and battery behaviour still affect reporting, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What does parent-controlled mean in Guardian?

Guardian keeps child-device location information within the parent-managed family for trusted adults rather than publishing it through a public social profile.

Should trusted-adult access be reviewed over time?

Yes. Families should reconsider access when caregiving arrangements or other circumstances change so information remains limited to people who still need it.

Does parent control make Guardian location perfectly accurate?

No. Phone GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery behaviour and operating-system restrictions can still affect location and update timing.
