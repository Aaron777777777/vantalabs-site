Practical checks when a child phone stops updating

When a Guardian location does not update, start with the device context rather than assuming the child has stopped moving. Check the connected phone’s last-update information and battery status to see how recently Guardian appears to have received a report.

Google PlayApp Store

Check the phone before assuming the journey stopped

Next, consider the phone conditions that location reporting depends on. Connectivity, location permission, background access and battery-saving restrictions can all prevent or delay a newer position reaching the Family Map.

If the child device is available, confirm that its normal location and background permissions are still enabled and that it has mobile or Wi-Fi connectivity. Restarting the app or phone can also help after temporary device problems.

When the information is important, contacting the family member directly is more appropriate than waiting for the map to refresh. Guardian cannot guarantee location updates and is not an emergency service.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
