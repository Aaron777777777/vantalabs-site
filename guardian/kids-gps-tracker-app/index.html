Understanding GPS tracking on a child’s phone

The last-update time is therefore important. If the timestamp is older than expected, the phone may have lost connectivity, had background activity restricted, changed location permissions or entered a battery-saving state.

Battery level adds another clue when parents are interpreting an older position. Large buildings, weak mobile coverage and ordinary GPS variation can also affect how close the reported point is to the phone’s real position.

Parents should use that context alongside normal family contact rather than treating a phone map as perfect proof. Guardian provides family location tools and is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What does Guardian show when a child phone has not reported recently?

The Family Map may continue to show the most recent location Guardian received, so the last-update information helps parents judge how old that position is.

Why can a kids GPS tracker show an older position?

Weak connectivity, background restrictions, changed permissions or battery-saving behaviour can interrupt normal location reporting.

Can buildings affect a child phone’s GPS position?

Yes. Indoor environments and large buildings can reduce GPS accuracy, so a reported map point should be treated as useful phone-location context rather than exact proof.
