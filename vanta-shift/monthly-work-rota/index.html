Designed around the employee’s week

The value of monthly work rota comes from turning the employee’s own rota, working days and upcoming scheduled shifts into a clearer personal calendar. The app provides a simpler way to keep a personal view of assigned shifts available on a mobile device, while keeping official rota and payroll responsibilities with the employer.

A monthly rota gives a broader view of work, rest days and repeating patterns across the complete calendar month. The employee should still check unusual changes or missing information against the latest employer-issued rota.

Your rota remains a personal planning view. The employer remains the source of the official rota and any subsequent changes.
