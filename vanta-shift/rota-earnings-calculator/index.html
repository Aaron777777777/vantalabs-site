A clearer view of the working week

Rota Earnings Calculator is centred entirely on the employee’s own assigned schedule, reminders, hours and estimated earnings. Vanta Shift is designed to form a clearer personal estimate of what scheduled shifts may earn. The information stays centred on the employee’s own work schedule.

The app is most useful when the displayed pattern, assigned shifts and pay settings reflect the employee’s real arrangements.

Estimated pay remains a personal planning view. Actual pay can differ because of payroll rules, deductions, premiums, overtime and employer-held information.
