One family routine, with room for individual children

Daily family life often includes a mixture of shared jobs and personal responsibilities. Parent Quest lets each child have a list that reflects their own part.

The parent can still see and manage the wider routine from one device, making it easier to adjust tasks when schedules or needs change.

Fair does not always mean identical. Different ages and abilities may call for different chores within the same family.

Quick questions

Do all chores have to happen every day?

No. Parents choose which jobs belong in the daily routine and which should stay occasional.

Can different children have different routines?

Yes. Each child profile can have its own chores and expectations.

Who decides whether a chore is complete?

The parent or guardian reviews the child’s progress through the approval flow.
