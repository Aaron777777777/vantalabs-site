Quick questions

What can I keep in my personal shift calendar?

You can keep your own shift dates, start and finish times, days off and notes together without managing anyone else's schedule.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does personal shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
