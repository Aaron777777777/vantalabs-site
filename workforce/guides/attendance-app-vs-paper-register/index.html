The practical thinking behind attendance app vs paper register

Attendance App vs Paper Register is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should book on when work begins and book off when the working period ends.

Authorised managers can then use booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history to see whether the record is complete, plausible and ready for review. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for attendance app vs paper register?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for attendance app vs paper register?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
