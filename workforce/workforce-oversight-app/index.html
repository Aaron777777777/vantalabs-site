What the organisation actually needs to see

Workforce oversight can bring structure to a process that otherwise depends heavily on memory, messages or manual reconciliation. Workers mainly need having relevant attendance and check actions reflected accurately. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess which workers are active and which records require attention, using worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks and the surrounding workplace context. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents making decisions from stale, incomplete or misunderstood device data from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce oversight?

The relevant view can include worker status, workplace activity and authorised management checks, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
