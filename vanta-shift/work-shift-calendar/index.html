A practical view of changing shifts

The value of work shift calendar comes from turning assigned work shifts, upcoming working days and the employee’s personal schedule into a clearer personal calendar. The employee can use Vanta Shift to see what shift comes next without relying on screenshots, messages or a paper rota. Its purpose stays limited to the employee’s own assigned schedule and personal totals.

The employee should still check unusual changes or missing information against the latest employer-issued rota.

Shift calendar remains a personal planning view. The calendar represents scheduled work and does not prove that a shift was completed.
