Place alerts for the moments that matter

A configured boundary can provide an arrival or leaving notification from the connected child phone, giving parents a useful milestone without requiring them to follow the whole journey on the Family Map.

The alert depends on the location readings the phone is able to provide. Buildings, weak signal, permissions, background restrictions and battery settings can all change when the place event is reported.

Place alerts are therefore useful family context rather than perfectly immediate or guaranteed events. Location information stays inside the private Guardian family, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Which family places are useful for Guardian alerts?

Home and School are clear everyday examples because their arrival and leaving events often match familiar family routines.

Do place alerts mean parents need to follow the map as well?

No. The alert can provide the useful milestone, while the Family Map is available only when a parent needs more context.

Can phone conditions change the timing of a place alert?

Yes. Signal, location permissions, background restrictions, GPS conditions and battery settings can all affect reporting.
