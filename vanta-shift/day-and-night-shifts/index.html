Keep the next shift easy to find

For employees working changing patterns, day and night shifts can make the week ahead much easier to read. The main benefit is being able to understand how days, nights and rest days repeat across future weeks. That personal view can support planning around work, rest days and other commitments.

Employees should treat the app as a personal view of the information available rather than the source of workplace policy.
