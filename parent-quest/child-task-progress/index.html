Child Task Progress

Follow each child’s task progress separately so completed work and outstanding jobs are easier to understand.

Google Play App Store

See where each child is in their own routine

A general family total does not tell you whether one child has finished everything while another still has several jobs waiting. Individual progress provides a more useful picture.

Parents can review submitted tasks and see confirmed progress within the correct child profile. That reduces mix-ups and makes conversations about unfinished work more specific.

Separate progress can also make review more useful when several children are completing chores at the same time. The parent can focus on one child’s submitted and approved work without a sibling’s tasks being mixed into the same view.

Progress belongs to the individual child. One child’s completed tasks do not alter another child’s record.

Quick questions

Is task progress tracked separately for each child profile?

Yes. Each child’s tasks and confirmed progress stay associated with that profile so parents can review children individually.

Does viewing child progress require a separate child account?

No. Progress is organised inside the parent-managed family setup, without requiring children to have independent accounts or logins.

How much identifying information does task progress need?

Only what the parent finds useful for distinguishing the child profile. A simple first name or nickname may be sufficient.
