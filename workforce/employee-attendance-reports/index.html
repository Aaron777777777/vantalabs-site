Connect the worker action with the later review

When employee attendance reports is handled consistently, the resulting record is easier for both workers and authorised managers to understand. For the worker, the expected action is to keep booking activity complete so the later report reflects the working period. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to select the correct workers and dates, then review exceptions before export, using attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports alongside any missing bookings, corrections or unusual working periods. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Attendance reporting and exports still needs sensible review. Reports and exports are operational records and require verification before further use.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee attendance reports?

The available record can include attendance history, monthly periods, hours and authorised exports, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for employee attendance reports?

Reports are operational records and should be verified before payroll, accounting, legal or employment decisions.
