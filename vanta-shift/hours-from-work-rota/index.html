Quick questions

How can I calculate hours from the rota I have entered?

Vanta Shift uses the shift times in your personal calendar to help you review how your scheduled hours are adding up across the rota.

Can I edit one date in a repeating pattern?

Yes. Individual shifts can be adjusted when a particular day differs from the usual rotation.

Does hours from a work rota manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
