School safe-zone alerts for calmer mornings and afternoons

A Guardian School safe zone represents the school as a practical area so the connected child phone can support arrival and leaving alerts without relying on one exact coordinate.

How a School safe zone supports routine alerts

The area can reflect the way the school is actually used, including entrances and grounds where normal GPS variation may move the reported phone position around a large building.

When the phone appears to cross the configured boundary, Guardian can provide a place-based update to trusted adults. The Family Map remains available if the alert needs more context.

Safe-zone timing can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions, so an alert may not match the exact moment a person crosses the school gate. Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

What is a School safe zone in Guardian?

It is a configured area around the school that Guardian can use with the connected child phone’s available location reports to support arrival and leaving alerts.

Why should a School safe zone be an area rather than one exact map point?

Entrances, grounds, large buildings and normal GPS drift make a practical area more useful than expecting a phone to report one precise coordinate.

Can a School safe-zone alert occur later than the actual gate crossing?

Yes. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery behaviour can affect when the phone reports the boundary event.
