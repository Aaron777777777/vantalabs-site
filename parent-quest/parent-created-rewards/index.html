Keep rewards personal instead of preselected

A reward chosen by the parent is more likely to fit the family’s rules, budget and routines. It might cost nothing at all, such as choosing a game, staying up a little later or helping plan the weekend.

Because you create the list yourself, it can grow with your children. Old rewards can be removed and new ones added without waiting for an app catalogue to catch up.

Vanta Labs does not supply rewards. Parent Quest records the family’s choices; fulfilling each reward remains the responsibility of the parent or guardian.

Quick questions

Who creates the rewards?

The parent or guardian creates and provides every reward.

Are rewards supplied by Vanta Labs?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee family rewards.

Can rewards be changed?

Yes. Parents can change the reward list as the family’s interests and circumstances change.
