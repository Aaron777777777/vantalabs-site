Keep the important decisions under adult control

Children need a clear way to see what is expected, but the family rules should still be managed by the parent. That includes assigning chores, checking completed work and deciding what progress is awarded.

Parent Quest keeps those controls together behind the Parent PIN. The child-facing view stays straightforward while the adult remains responsible for the setup and final approval.

This separation is useful on a shared parent-owned device because children can still interact with their tasks without gaining access to the settings that define the routine. Chore assignment, approval and reward decisions remain part of the parent-controlled side.

Protected actions stay with the parent. Keep the Parent PIN private so children cannot change the routine or approve their own work.

Quick questions

Why does a parent-controlled chore app use a Parent PIN?

The PIN separates protected adult controls from the simpler child-facing task view on the shared device.

Can children use the app without being able to approve chores?

Yes. Children can view and complete their tasks while approval remains a protected parent action.

Does keeping approval parent-controlled make the routine complicated?

No. Parent controls stay separate, but the actual approval step is intended to remain quick and practical.
