child GPS tracking

A child GPS tracker app with useful context

Guardian uses the connected child phone as the source of family location information. The Family Map shows the location the phone has been able to report rather than pretending the device is broadcasting a flawless continuous position.

A calmer way to stay informed

Home and School alerts can cover familiar journey milestones, while battery level and last-update information give parents extra context when the map looks older than expected or an alert appears late.

A child GPS tracker works best when families understand the phone conditions behind it. Location permissions, connectivity, background activity, buildings and battery restrictions can all influence accuracy and update timing.

Guardian is intended to reduce unnecessary checking, not replace direct communication or prove a child’s exact physical position. It is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Where does Guardian get child GPS location information from?

Guardian relies on location information reported by the connected child phone using that device’s available location services and permissions.

Does a child GPS tracker need to be watched throughout the day?

No. Home and School alerts can provide useful routine milestones, while the Family Map remains available when additional context is needed.

Why might a child GPS position look older than expected?

Connectivity, background restrictions, permissions, battery conditions and other phone factors can delay when a newer location reaches Guardian.
