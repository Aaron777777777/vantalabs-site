Useful school-run updates, without watching the map

A useful boundary should represent the place as an area rather than one exact coordinate. Home may need to account for the building and immediate surroundings, while School may include entrances, grounds and normal GPS movement around a larger site.

Once those places are configured, arrival and leaving alerts can cover routine milestones while the Family Map remains available for occasions when parents need more context.

Families should expect some variation because GPS, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can affect safe-zone detection. These alerts support family routines rather than provide guaranteed movement records, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Why are Home and School useful first safe zones to set up?

They represent familiar places with clear everyday arrival and leaving milestones that many families already use to organise their routines.

Should a Home or School safe zone be one exact GPS point?

No. A practical area can better account for buildings, entrances, grounds and normal phone-location variation.

Do configured safe zones remove the need to check the Family Map?

Often the alerts may provide enough routine information, but the Family Map remains available when plans change or additional context is needed.
