Build a rota that makes sense at a glance

For employees working changing patterns, rota planner app can make the week ahead much easier to read. The main benefit is being able to keep a personal view of assigned shifts available on a mobile device. That personal view can support planning around work, rest days and other commitments.

It supports personal planning rather than replacing formal employer records.

Your rota remains a personal planning view. The employer remains the source of the official rota and any subsequent changes.

Quick questions

What does rota planner app show?

It focuses on the employee’s own rota, working days and upcoming scheduled shifts, using the shift information available in Vanta Shift.

What is Vanta Shift intended to help with?

Vanta Shift helps employees view assigned shifts, receive notifications and review scheduled hours and estimated pay.

Should I still check the official rota?

The employer remains the source of the official rota and any subsequent changes.
