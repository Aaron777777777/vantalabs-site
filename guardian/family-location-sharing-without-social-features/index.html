Quick questions

Does Guardian use public profiles or follower-style location sharing?

No. Connected child-device information stays within the private Guardian family rather than being exposed through public profiles or social discovery.

Who should have access to private family location information?

Access should be limited to trusted adults the family has chosen to include and reviewed whenever those arrangements change.

Can an adult participate in the Guardian family without sharing their own position?

Yes. Adult location sharing is optional and is separate from access to authorised child-device information.
