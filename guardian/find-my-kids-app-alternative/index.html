Quick questions

What does Guardian offer to someone looking for a Find My Kids alternative?

Guardian offers its own private Family Map, Home and School alerts, device context, recent location information and a child-device SOS option.

Does this page rank Guardian above Find My Kids?

No. Different families value different features, so it is sensible to compare the current services against the routines and privacy choices that matter to your family.

Does Guardian promise perfectly live child location?

No. Guardian relies on phone location reporting, which can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
