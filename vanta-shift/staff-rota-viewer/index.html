Quick questions

Does Vanta Shift let me view the rota I personally work?

Yes. You can keep your own working dates and shift times in the app, but Vanta Shift does not provide a manager view of other staff rotas.

Are calculated totals official records?

No. Hours and earnings shown by the app are personal planning figures based on the information entered.

Does staff rota viewer manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
