Plan the pattern you actually work

Personal Rota Planner brings the employee’s own rota, working days and upcoming scheduled shifts into one place so the next working day is easier to see. A clear mobile calendar can help the employee keep a personal view of assigned shifts available on a mobile device, especially when shifts repeat or change across the month.

Employees should treat the app as a personal view of the information available rather than the source of workplace policy.

Your rota remains a personal planning view. The employer remains the source of the official rota and any subsequent changes.

Quick questions

What does personal rota planner show?

It focuses on the employee’s own rota, working days and upcoming scheduled shifts, using the shift information available in Vanta Shift.

What is Vanta Shift intended to help with?

Vanta Shift helps employees view assigned shifts, receive notifications and review scheduled hours and estimated pay.

Should I still check the official rota?

The employer remains the source of the official rota and any subsequent changes.
