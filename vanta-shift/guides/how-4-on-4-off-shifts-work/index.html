Quick questions

What is a 4 on 4 off shift pattern?

It is a repeating work-and-rest pattern. Understanding the cycle and your starting point makes it easier to lay the rota out in Vanta Shift.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. Start and finish times can cross midnight so overnight work remains clear in the calendar.

Does how 4 on 4 off shifts work manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
