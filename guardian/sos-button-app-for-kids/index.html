SOS button kids

A simple SOS button for a connected child device

The Guardian SOS button gives a connected child device a simple way to ask trusted family adults for attention through the app. It is intended to be easy to understand without pretending that one button can replace a family emergency plan.

An extra way to reach trusted adults

When SOS is used, Guardian can provide the family with an alert and available context from the child phone, including its latest reported location where that information is available.

Parents can explain clearly what the button is for and what it is not for. A child should know which adults may respond, what to do if nobody responds and when the situation requires direct emergency help instead.

Connectivity, permissions, battery conditions and other phone restrictions can affect alert delivery and location reporting. Guardian cannot guarantee an SOS notification and is not an emergency service.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What is the Guardian SOS button intended to do?

It gives the connected child device a simple way to send an SOS alert to trusted adults in the private Guardian family.

Should a child treat the SOS button as guaranteed emergency contact?

No. App notification delivery cannot be guaranteed, so families should teach children what other action to take if a situation is urgent.

Can phone settings affect a Guardian SOS button alert?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery restrictions and other device conditions can affect notification and location reporting.
