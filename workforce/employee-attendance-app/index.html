Turning daily activity into a usable record

The question behind employee attendance app is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? The app supplies the mobile action; the business supplies the expectation. Here that means a clear booking action at the beginning and end of attendance, together with a known support route.

For an authorised manager, the useful question is whether the attendance record is complete and needs correction. Vanta Workforce keeps booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods together so the review does not depend on collecting separate updates. Good records begin before anyone opens the app. The organisation should set clear expectations and establish how forgotten bookings, delayed records or unusual attendance durations will be handled.

What to keep in mind Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee attendance app?

The relevant view can include booking activity, attendance history and recorded working periods, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.
