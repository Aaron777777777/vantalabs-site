The practical thinking behind how to introduce digital attendance

How to Introduce Digital Attendance is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should book on when work begins and book off when the working period ends.

Authorised managers can then use booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history to see whether the record is complete, plausible and ready for review. Before rollout, the business should agree who reviews exceptions, how corrections are requested and what fallback applies when the app cannot be used.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for how to introduce digital attendance?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Should the process be explained before launch?

Yes. Workers should understand what is recorded, why it is needed and how genuine exceptions are handled.

What limitation matters most for how to introduce digital attendance?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
