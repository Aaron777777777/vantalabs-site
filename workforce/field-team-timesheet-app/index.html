Build a record that managers can review properly

A useful approach to field team timesheets begins with one straightforward question: what does the worker need to do, and what does the manager need to review? A clear process tells the worker to follow the same booking process while working across sites or field locations, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to review distributed attendance without collecting separate messages from each site. The relevant evidence includes off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for field team timesheets?

The available record can include off-site bookings, workplace details and recorded working hours, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for field team timesheets?

The organisation still needs a fallback process for weak signal, device problems and unclear site information.
