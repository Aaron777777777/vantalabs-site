Make the process easier to follow and review

Workplace Incident Reporting App works best when the organisation starts with the real daily routine and uses the app to make that routine easier to record. For the worker, the expected action is to respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to see which checks are complete and which require a human response, using scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information alongside the time, location and supporting information recorded for the incident. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workplace incident reporting?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for workplace incident reporting?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
