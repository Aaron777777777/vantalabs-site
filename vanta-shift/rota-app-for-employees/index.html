Quick questions

What is Personal Rota App useful for?

It helps you check upcoming work without searching through screenshots or messages using the personal rota stored on your device.

Can I change the information later?

Yes. Shifts, times, notes and patterns can be adjusted when your real schedule changes.

Does Personal Rota App manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
