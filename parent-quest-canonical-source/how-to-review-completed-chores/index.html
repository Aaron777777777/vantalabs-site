How to Review Completed Chores

Check completed chores quickly and fairly so approval supports the routine instead of becoming another source of conflict.

Google Play App Store

Agree what finished means beforehand

Children are more likely to accept a review when expectations were clear before they started. Use specific task names and explain any important standard in simple terms.

When reviewing the work, recognise genuine effort and point out what still needs doing without turning every small job into an inspection. Approve the task when it meets the family’s agreed expectation.

Keep the review proportionate. A simple task usually needs a simple check, not a lengthy examination.

Quick questions

Should every family use the same chore routine?

No. Chores and rewards should suit the individual child and household.

How many chores should parents start with?

A small, realistic list is usually easier to explain and maintain.

Can the routine be changed later?

Yes. Parents should review what works and adjust anything that creates unnecessary friction.
