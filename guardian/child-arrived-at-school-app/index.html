Quick questions

What triggers a Guardian School arrival notification?

The update is based on the connected child phone entering the family’s configured School safe zone.

Can large School buildings affect location timing?

Yes. GPS conditions, buildings, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions can all affect when the phone reports its location.

Can people outside the Guardian family see the School location?

No. Guardian is designed around private family access rather than public profiles or a public location feed.
