Work Hours and Pay App is useful when an employee wants a clearer personal view of estimated earnings calculated from available shift and hourly rate information. The main benefit is being able to form a clearer personal estimate of what scheduled shifts may earn. That personal view can support planning around work, rest days and other commitments.

Clear notifications and accurate shift details depend on the latest information being available in the app.

Estimated pay remains a personal planning view. Actual pay can differ because of payroll rules, deductions, premiums, overtime and employer-held information.
