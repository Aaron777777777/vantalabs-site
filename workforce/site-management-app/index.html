From worker action to useful review

Site management can bring structure to a process that otherwise depends heavily on memory, messages or manual reconciliation. For the worker, the important part is seeing only the actions and information relevant to their role. The organisation should explain what counts as completion and what to do when a device, account or connection problem prevents the normal action.

Authorised managers can use the available record to examine whether sites, access and worker records match the real organisation. That evidence includes workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system and should be read with its timing and workplace circumstances. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents unclear ownership of setup, corrections or ongoing administration from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

What to keep in mind The organisation remains responsible for user access, workplace configuration and policy.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for site management?

The relevant view can include workplaces, workers, roles and attendance activity in one operational system, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
