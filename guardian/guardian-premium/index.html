Premium support for additional child profiles

Guardian keeps its family-location experience focused on private access, useful location context and familiar-place updates. The core setup supports a connected child profile, while Guardian Premium is intended for families that need support for additional child profiles. The aim is to extend the same parent-managed family experience rather than introduce public or social tracking features.

This page does not introduce new pricing claims or unsupported Premium features. Guardian is available on Android through Google Play and on iPhone through the App Store. Location information still depends on the connected phones and can be affected by GPS, signal, permissions, connectivity, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions, regardless of plan.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Who is Guardian Premium intended for?

Guardian Premium is intended for families that need support for additional child profiles.

Does Guardian Premium turn Guardian into a public tracking service?

No. Guardian remains a private, parent-managed family-location app rather than a public or social location network.

Do phone-location limitations still apply with Guardian Premium?

Yes. GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery conditions, buildings and operating-system restrictions can still affect location reporting.
