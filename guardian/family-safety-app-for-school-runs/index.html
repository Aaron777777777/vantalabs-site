Quick questions

Which parts of the school run can Guardian help parents follow?

Home and School alerts can provide useful milestones such as leaving Home, reaching School, leaving School and returning Home.

Does a school-run alert prove exactly where the child is?

No. The alert reflects location information reported by the connected phone and should not be treated as proof of an exact physical position.

What if a school-run update appears late?

Phone signal, GPS conditions, permissions, background restrictions and battery settings can all affect reporting, so parents may need to use direct communication as well.
