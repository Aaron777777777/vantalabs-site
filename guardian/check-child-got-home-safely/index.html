Quick questions

Can Guardian provide a Home update without continuous map watching?

Yes. A configured Home safe zone can provide an arrival notification while the Family Map remains available only when extra context is needed.

Does a Home arrival alert guarantee that everything is safe?

No. It reflects the connected phone reaching the Home safe zone and should not be treated as a guarantee of a person’s safety.

What should a parent do if the expected Home update does not appear?

Check available device context and contact the child directly. If there may be immediate danger, contact the emergency services rather than relying on Guardian.
