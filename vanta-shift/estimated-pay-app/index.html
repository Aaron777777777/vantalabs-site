A clearer view of the working week

Estimated Pay App is centred entirely on the employee’s own assigned schedule, reminders, hours and estimated earnings. A clear mobile calendar can help the employee form a clearer personal estimate of what scheduled shifts may earn, especially when shifts repeat or change across the month.

Employees should treat the app as a personal view of the information available rather than the source of workplace policy.

Estimated pay remains a personal planning view. Actual pay can differ because of payroll rules, deductions, premiums, overtime and employer-held information.
