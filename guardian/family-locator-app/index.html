family location

A private family locator app without the social noise

Guardian works as a private family locator by keeping connected devices inside one parent-managed family rather than turning location sharing into a public or social experience.

Google PlayApp Store

One private view for connected family devices

Trusted adults can check the latest available child-device location together with battery and update context. Home and School alerts can cover routine milestones so the map does not need to become an all-day dashboard.

Parents can also decide whether their own location should be shared. Adult location sharing remains optional and separate from the connected child device’s location features.

Like any phone-based location service, Guardian depends on GPS, connectivity, permissions and device settings. The information can be useful for family coordination but cannot provide perfect accuracy or replace emergency services.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Does Guardian put family locations on a public social map?

No. Guardian keeps connected-device information inside the private, parent-managed family for trusted adults with access.

Can parents use Guardian without sharing their own location?

Yes. Adult location sharing is optional and does not have to be enabled to use child-device location features.

Can a family locator app always show a perfectly accurate position?

No. GPS, signal, permissions, battery settings and other phone conditions can affect location accuracy and update timing.
