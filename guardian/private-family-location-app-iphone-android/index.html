Private Family Location App for iPhone and Android, built around useful family context

Families often use a mix of phones rather than one platform. Guardian is available through the App Store and Google Play so supported iPhone and Android devices can take part in the same family safety routine, subject to the permissions and background-location behaviour of each device.

Platform settings can affect how reliably a phone reports in the background, so location permissions, battery optimisation and connectivity still matter. Guardian keeps the resulting family information private to authorised family members and provides last-update context when a device has not reported recently.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
