Quick questions

How does a repeating rota work in Vanta Shift?

Choose the sequence and starting point that match your rota and Vanta Shift can place that repeating pattern across your personal calendar.

Are calculated totals official records?

No. Hours and earnings shown by the app are personal planning figures based on the information entered.

Does repeating rota calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
