Quick questions

Can I set a reminder for my next shift?

Yes. You can use a reminder around the next shift saved in your personal calendar to give yourself an extra prompt before its scheduled start.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does next shift reminder manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
