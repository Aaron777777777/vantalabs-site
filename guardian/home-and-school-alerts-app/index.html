One place for Home and School arrival alerts

Guardian Home and School alerts focus on four useful routine milestones: a connected child phone leaving Home, arriving at School, leaving School and returning Home.

Google PlayApp Store

Useful school-run updates, without watching the map

Those safe-zone events can give parents a clearer picture of the school day without turning the Family Map into something that needs to stay open throughout every journey.

When an expected update is late or plans change, the latest available phone-location context, battery information and update status can help parents understand what the device has most recently reported.

The alerts still depend on GPS, connectivity, permissions and phone settings, so they can be delayed or inaccurate. Guardian supports everyday family coordination but cannot guarantee a journey outcome and is not an emergency service.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

Which Home and School milestones can Guardian alert parents about?

Guardian can provide place-based updates for leaving Home, arriving at School, leaving School and returning Home.

Do Home and School alerts remove the need for the Family Map?

Not entirely. Routine alerts may provide enough information most days, while the Family Map remains available when extra context is useful.

Are Home and School notifications guaranteed to arrive at the exact moment of movement?

No. GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and phone restrictions can affect both detection and notification timing.
