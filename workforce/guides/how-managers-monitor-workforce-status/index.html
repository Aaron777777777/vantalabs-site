The practical thinking behind how managers monitor workforce status

How Managers Monitor Workforce Status is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app.

Authorised managers can then use worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for how managers monitor workforce status?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for how managers monitor workforce status?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
