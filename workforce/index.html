Know who is working and keep the record together

Vanta Workforce keeps attendance activity, workplace presence and recorded hours in one connected system. Workers have a direct booking-on and booking-off flow, while authorised managers can review the information relevant to their organisation.

Attendance records continue into My Hours, monthly periods and timesheet exports. Scheduled prompts, worker phone checks and incident reporting sit alongside the same operational record without pretending that an app removes the need for human review.

Useful workforce records still require sensible review. Location, attendance and current status can be affected by connectivity, permissions, device conditions and incomplete worker actions. Vanta Workforce is not an emergency service or an automatic payroll system.

Quick questions

Who is Vanta Workforce built for?

It is built for organisations that need mobile attendance, workplace records, timesheets and relevant worker checks for managers and workers.

Does it track workers continuously?

No. Location provides context around relevant app and attendance actions. Its availability can be affected by permissions, signal and device settings.

Does Vanta Workforce process payroll automatically?

No. Timesheets and exports support further business review but should be checked before payroll, accounting or employment use.
