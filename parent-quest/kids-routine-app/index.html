Make the day easier to recognise

Children often feel more confident when the routine is familiar. A visible list can help with mornings, after-school jobs and getting ready for bed.

The parent decides which tasks belong in the routine and can change them whenever the child’s needs or schedule change.

Routines still need explanation and support. The app helps children see the steps, but parents remain responsible for guidance and supervision.

Quick questions

Can the family routine be changed later?

Yes. Parents can adjust chores, rewards and expectations whenever family life changes.

Do rewards have to be used for every chore?

No. Parents decide when rewards are useful and when chores are simply part of family life.

Does Parent Quest replace parental supervision?

No. It helps organise the routine but does not replace parental judgement or supervision.
