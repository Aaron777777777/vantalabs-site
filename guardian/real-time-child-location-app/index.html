Quick questions

What does real-time child location mean in Guardian?

It means Guardian can show the latest location successfully reported by the connected child phone, with newer updates arriving when the device is able to provide them.

Is Guardian a perfectly continuous live child-location feed?

No. Connectivity, GPS, permissions, background restrictions, battery behaviour and operating-system conditions can all create gaps or delays.

How can parents tell whether the child map position may be old?

Battery information and the device’s last-update context help parents judge how recently the connected phone appears to have reported.
