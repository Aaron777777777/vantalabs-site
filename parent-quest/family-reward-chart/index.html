Keep family rewards visible and fair

Reward systems become confusing when the rules keep changing or nobody remembers what was agreed. A shared chart makes the available choices easier to see and gives everyone the same starting point.

Parents set the rewards and decide how many coins are needed. Each child’s progress remains separate, so one child’s completed chores do not get mixed up with somebody else’s.

The chart can therefore show clear goals without creating a fixed contract that never changes. Parents remain free to keep rewards realistic for the household, explain any changes and replace options that no longer fit the family.

Fair does not have to mean identical. Parents can choose rewards and goals that suit each child rather than forcing every sibling into the same arrangement.

Quick questions

Who puts the rewards on the family reward chart?

The parent or guardian chooses the rewards shown on the chart and provides them for the family.

Are family reward-chart rewards provided by Vanta Labs?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee the rewards a parent chooses for a family chart.

Can the family reward chart be updated over time?

Yes. Parents can change the available rewards as children grow, interests move on or household circumstances change.
