Quick questions

What should parents compare when choosing a child tracker app?

Compare family access controls, useful place alerts, device and update context, privacy choices and how realistically the app explains phone-location limits.

Does the best child tracker need constant map watching?

Not necessarily. Place-based alerts can cover routine milestones while the map remains available when a parent genuinely needs more context.

Can any child tracker app guarantee an exact location at all times?

No. Phone GPS, connectivity, permissions, buildings, battery conditions and operating-system restrictions can all affect location reporting.
