Turn a daily action into something useful later

The value of Staff Status Dashboard becomes clear when the same information would otherwise be scattered across messages, paper records and memory. From the worker’s perspective, success means being able to complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app without navigating unnecessary management tools.

For an authorised manager, the useful outcome is the ability to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it. The available view brings together worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records. The technology is strongest when expectations, ownership and correction routes have already been made clear. For Staff Status Dashboard, that review should stay focused on the specific record rather than assumptions about the wider working day.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for staff status dashboard?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for staff status dashboard?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
