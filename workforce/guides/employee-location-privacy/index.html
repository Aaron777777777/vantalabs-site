The practical thinking behind employee location privacy

Employee Location Privacy is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking.

Authorised managers can then use booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee location privacy?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for employee location privacy?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
