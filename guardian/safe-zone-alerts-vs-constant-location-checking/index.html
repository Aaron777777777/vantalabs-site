alerts vs constant checking

Why milestone alerts can be healthier than watching a map

Safe-zone alerts and constant map checking solve different problems. Guardian uses place alerts to focus attention on useful milestones rather than encouraging parents to follow every movement of a connected child phone.

Alerts can replace many unnecessary map checks

For a familiar journey, leaving Home, reaching School, leaving School or returning Home may provide enough information. The Family Map can stay in reserve for an unexpected delay or change of plans.

That alert-first approach can make family location sharing feel more proportionate while still giving parents access to additional context when they genuinely need it.

Safe-zone notifications can still be affected by GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions. They are not guaranteed or perfectly immediate, and Guardian is not an emergency service.

Quick questions

Why use safe-zone alerts instead of checking a map repeatedly?

Place alerts can provide the familiar milestones a parent cares about while keeping the Family Map available for occasions that need extra context.

Does using alerts mean the Family Map is unavailable?

No. The Family Map remains available when an alert is late, plans change or a parent wants a closer look at the latest location context.

Can safe-zone alerts remove all uncertainty from a journey?

No. Phone location and notification timing can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions.
