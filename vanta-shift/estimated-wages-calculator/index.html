Quick questions

What can I use the estimated wages calculator for?

You can use the shifts and pay information you enter to form a personal wages estimate and review how expected earnings may build across your rota.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does estimated wages calculator manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
