Make the process easier to follow and review

Employee Location Clock-In works best when the organisation starts with the real daily routine and uses the app to make that routine easier to record. For the worker, the expected action is to allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking. That instruction should be explained during onboarding, including what to do when a phone, account or connection problem gets in the way.

The manager does not merely need a list of taps. They need to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace, using booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device together with the quality and timing of the available location information. A short test with the real team is worthwhile before the process becomes part of everyday operations.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for employee location clock-in?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Do workers and managers see identical information?

Not necessarily. Available screens and actions depend on the authorised role assigned to the user.

What limitation matters most for employee location clock-in?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
