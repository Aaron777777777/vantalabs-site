Quick questions

How does the rota earnings calculator use my shifts?

It uses the shift and pay information you enter in your personal rota to form an estimated earnings figure for your own planning.

Who controls the information in the app?

The person using the device enters and maintains their own rota, times and pay details.

Does rota earnings calculator manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
