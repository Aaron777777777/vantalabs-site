Quick questions

What is a shift cycle calendar useful for?

It helps you see the order of your repeating working and rest days and where the current date sits within the cycle you have entered.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does shift cycle calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
