Quick questions

What is shift calendar app useful for?

It helps you see working days, rest days and changing times together using the personal shift calendar stored on your device.

Can I change the information later?

Yes. Shifts, times, notes and patterns can be adjusted when your real schedule changes.

Does shift calendar app manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
