A practical process for everyday operations

The question behind mobile workforce management is straightforward: how can the business create a clearer record without adding unnecessary work? The worker-facing process centres on a consistent attendance process from a supported mobile device. It should remain understandable during busy arrivals, departures, site moves and scheduled checks.

For an authorised manager, the useful question is how attendance is recorded across changing sites and field locations. Vanta Workforce keeps off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history together so the review does not depend on collecting separate updates. Before launch, the business should document the routine and the correction process. That prevents weak connectivity or unclear expectations producing inconsistent records from becoming an automatic assumption about the worker.

What to keep in mind Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for mobile workforce management?

The relevant view can include off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.
