Earn Coins for Chores

Let selected chores contribute reward coins towards a family-made goal after the parent has checked they are complete.

Google Play App Store

Help regular chores build towards a bigger reward

A child may be working towards something that would not make sense as the reward for one quick job. Coins allow several chores to contribute gradually instead of turning every task into a separate prize.

Parents decide which chores earn coins and which remain normal family responsibilities. That flexibility helps keep the system useful without attaching a reward to absolutely everything.

Not every chore needs a coin value. The parent chooses when reward coins are useful and when a task should simply remain part of the normal routine.

Quick questions

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are an in-app way to show progress and have no cash value.

Who decides how many coins a task is worth?

The parent controls how reward coins are used inside the family routine.

Can coins be exchanged with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be withdrawn, purchased from or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
