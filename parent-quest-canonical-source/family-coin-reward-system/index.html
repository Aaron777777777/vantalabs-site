Family Coin Reward System

Create one family coin system with separate progress for each child and rewards chosen by the parent.

Google Play App Store

Use shared rules without mixing everyone together

Families may want the same basic approach for all children while still giving each child their own tasks, balances and goals. That keeps the routine consistent without losing track of individual progress.

Parents control the coin values and reward choices from the same device. Each child’s profile keeps their approved progress separate, even when siblings are working towards similar rewards.

Each child keeps their own progress. Coins recorded for one child are not shared with or added to another child’s balance.

Quick questions

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are an in-app way to show progress and have no cash value.

Who decides how many coins a task is worth?

The parent controls how reward coins are used inside the family routine.

Can coins be exchanged with Vanta Labs?

No. Reward coins cannot be withdrawn, purchased from or exchanged with Vanta Labs.
