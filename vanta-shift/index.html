Your rota, hours and estimated earnings in one place

Vanta Shift gives individual shift workers a clear place to plan day shifts, night shifts and days off. Create a repeating pattern such as 4 on 4 off, 2 on 2 off or Panama, then change individual dates whenever real life changes.

Your calendar keeps scheduled hours, notes and estimated earnings close to the rota that created them. There are no staff accounts, manager controls, workplace tracking or unnecessary workforce-management features.

Your rota stays personal. Vanta Shift is a personal planner for individual shift workers. It is not an attendance system, staff scheduler, payroll service or clocking-in app.

Quick questions

Does Vanta Shift require an account?

No. It is designed as a straightforward personal shift planner without account setup.

Can I create my own shift pattern?

Yes. You can use common rota patterns or create a custom combination of day, night and off shifts.

Is Vanta Shift a workforce-management app?

No. It does not manage staff, attendance, clocking, payroll or employer operations.
