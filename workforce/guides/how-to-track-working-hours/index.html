The practical thinking behind how to track working hours

How to Track Working Hours is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should review recorded hours and flag anything that appears incomplete.

Authorised managers can then use booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods to check the underlying attendance periods before using a monthly total. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected.

Timesheets and working hours still needs sensible review. Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for how to track working hours?

The available record can include booked attendance, calculated durations and monthly working periods, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can an unusual record be reviewed?

Yes. Authorised managers can examine the available timestamps and context, then follow the organisation’s correction process.

What limitation matters most for how to track working hours?

Timesheets and exports require checking before payroll, accounting or employment use.
