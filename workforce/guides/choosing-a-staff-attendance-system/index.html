The practical thinking behind choosing a staff attendance system

Choosing a Staff Attendance System is easier to understand when the organisation first separates the worker action from the later management review. The worker should book on when work begins and book off when the working period ends.

Authorised managers can then use booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history to see whether the record is complete, plausible and ready for review. The technology is strongest when expectations, ownership and correction routes have already been made clear.

Staff attendance still needs sensible review. Attendance records should be reviewed before payroll, accounting or employment decisions.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for choosing a staff attendance system?

The available record can include booking times, recorded duration, workplace and attendance history, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

What happens if the normal app action cannot be completed?

The organisation should provide a clear fallback for genuine device, account, permission or connectivity problems.

What limitation matters most for choosing a staff attendance system?

A missed or delayed booking should be investigated rather than treated automatically as absence or misconduct.
