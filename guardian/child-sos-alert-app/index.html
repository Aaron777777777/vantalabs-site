What happens after a child presses SOS

The purpose of the alert is to help the family recognise that the child is asking for attention and decide what to do next. Depending on the situation, that may mean calling the child, contacting another trusted adult or taking further action outside Guardian.

The information attached to an SOS alert is still phone-based. GPS conditions, signal, location permissions, battery restrictions and connectivity can affect what is available and how quickly it is reported.

Notification delivery and location accuracy cannot be guaranteed. Guardian does not automatically contact emergency services and is not an emergency service; if somebody may be in immediate danger, emergency services should be contacted directly.

Quick questions

What can happen when a child activates SOS in Guardian?

Guardian can alert trusted family adults and include available location and device context from the connected child phone.

Does an SOS alert always include a perfectly accurate location?

No. The available location comes from the phone and can be affected by GPS conditions, connectivity, permissions and battery restrictions.

Does Guardian automatically contact emergency services after SOS is pressed?

No. Guardian alerts the family through its own SOS feature; it does not automatically contact emergency services and is not an emergency service.
