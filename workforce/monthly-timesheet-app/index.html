A practical process for everyday operations

The practical purpose of monthly timesheets is not simply to digitise a form. It is to produce a clearer record that remains useful after the working day has finished. Workers mainly need being able to review available hours and identify missing attendance. Clear onboarding matters because even a technically valid record can be misunderstood when the expected workplace process has not been explained.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess whether the recorded periods accurately describe the hours under review, using My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records and the surrounding workplace context. Rollout should include a short explanation, a test action and a known support route, particularly because of incomplete attendance being mistaken for a final payroll-ready total.

What to keep in mind Timesheets and exports must be checked before they are used outside Vanta Workforce.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for monthly timesheets?

The relevant view can include My Hours, monthly attendance periods and timesheet records, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.
