What the organisation should expect from the process

Businesses normally consider workforce oversight because the current process is slow to check, difficult to consolidate or too dependent on memory. A clear process tells the worker to complete attendance and check actions accurately from the supported app, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to review current worker status and focus attention on records that need it. The relevant evidence includes worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

Control Centre still needs sensible review. Managers remain responsible for reviewing timestamps, context and appropriate follow-up.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for workforce oversight?

The available record can include worker status, workplace activity, scheduled checks and attendance records, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for workforce oversight?

Managers must consider timestamps and workplace context before acting on incomplete or delayed information.
