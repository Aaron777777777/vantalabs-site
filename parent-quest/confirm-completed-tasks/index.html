Make confirmed progress easy to understand

Families need a clear difference between a task that has been submitted and one that has been accepted. Without that distinction, reward totals can quickly become a source of disagreement.

Parent confirmation closes the loop. Once you approve the task, the child can see that the work has counted and the routine can move on without uncertainty.

This can be especially useful when several tasks are being completed across the day. The child does not have to guess whether a submitted job counted, and the parent has a clear point at which the task changes from waiting for review to confirmed progress.

Confirmation creates the final record. Until the parent approves it, a completed task remains waiting for review.

Quick questions

Why are task confirmations kept behind the Parent PIN?

The PIN protects the step that turns submitted work into confirmed progress, helping keep that decision with the parent or guardian.

Can children confirm their own completed tasks?

No. Children can submit completed tasks, while confirmation remains an adult action.

Is confirming a finished task a long process?

No. It is designed as a short review step so parents can confirm progress without creating an unnecessarily complicated workflow.
