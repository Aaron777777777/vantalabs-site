Check a child's latest reported location, privately

When pickup arrangements change or a child is later than expected, Guardian gives trusted adults a private Family Map to check the latest location reported by the connected child phone. You can also see available battery and last-update context to judge how recent that information is.

What can parents use Guardian for?

  • Quick location checks: Open the Family Map when you need context about the child phone's most recent report.
  • Home and School updates: Receive arrival or departure alerts for familiar places, subject to normal device and network conditions.
  • Device context: Check the battery level and last-update time instead of assuming the map pin is live.
  • Private family access: Keep location sharing within the trusted family setup instead of a public social profile.
  • Extra SOS option: A connected child can ask the private family for urgent attention, but Guardian is not an emergency service.

What do you need to get started?

Your child needs a compatible connected phone, and the family needs to complete Guardian's setup and permission steps. Guardian is a phone-based tracking app, not a substitute for a standalone GPS device. Location accuracy and update timing depend on GPS, connectivity, background permissions and battery conditions.

See our child-phone setup guide, explore the private Family Map or compare GPS tracker apps for kids before choosing an option for your family. Use the Google Play or App Store links above to find Guardian.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

When is a quick Guardian location check useful?

It can be useful when a pickup changes, somebody is later than expected or a parent needs extra context about what the connected child phone last reported.

Can Home and School alerts reduce manual map checks?

Yes. Place alerts can handle many routine arrival and leaving updates so manual map checks can be reserved for times when more context is useful.

Who can access family location information in Guardian?

Access is limited to trusted adults within the private Guardian family rather than public or social profiles.
