Quick questions

How does Vanta Shift handle night shifts?

Night shifts can include their own start and finish times, including work that crosses midnight, so your personal rota stays clear across both dates.

Can I change the information later?

Yes. Shifts, times, notes and patterns can be adjusted when your real schedule changes.

Does night shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
