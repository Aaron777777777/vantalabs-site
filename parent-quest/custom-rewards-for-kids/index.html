Custom Rewards for Kids

Create rewards your children genuinely care about, from choosing the family film to a little extra screen time.

Google Play App Store

Use rewards that make sense in your home

You already know what matters to your children, so there is no need to pick from a generic catalogue. Add your own ideas, change them when interests move on and set goals that feel achievable.

Reward coins can show how close each child is to something they are working towards. The coins stay inside Parent Quest, have no cash value and are never sold or supplied by Vanta Labs.

The custom reward remains entirely separate from those in-app coins. A parent might choose extra screen time, picking the family film or another household privilege, but the parent is the one creating and providing that reward rather than Vanta Labs.

Every reward is family made. The parent or guardian chooses, manages and provides every reward shown in the app.

Quick questions

Who creates a custom reward for a child?

The parent or guardian creates the custom reward and is responsible for providing it within their own family.

Will Vanta Labs supply or pay for a custom reward?

No. Vanta Labs does not fund, supply or guarantee any custom reward entered by a parent.

Can custom rewards be replaced when a child’s interests change?

Yes. Parents can update or remove custom rewards whenever they stop fitting the child or family.
