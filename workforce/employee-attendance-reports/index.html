Where this fits inside Vanta Workforce

Businesses normally look for employee attendance reports when the working day is creating information in too many separate places. From the worker’s perspective, success means having recorded hours represented clearly and consistently. A fair exception route should also exist for genuine mistakes and device problems.

A manager needs more than a list of taps. They need to assess whether the selected workers, dates and periods are correct, using attendance history, monthly reports and authorised timesheet exports and the surrounding workplace context. The most reliable approach is to test the process with the team, explain the available records and review using an unchecked export as a final payroll or accounting conclusion proportionately.

Attendance reporting and exports still needs sensible review. Reports and exports are operational records and require verification before further use.
