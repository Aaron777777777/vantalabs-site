A departure alert for the journey after School

A School departure notification can help parents know that the next part of the day has begun—whether that means walking Home, meeting a pickup or travelling to an after-school activity.

Leaving a safe zone does not mean the child has reached the next destination. Guardian keeps the latest available map position, battery status and update time available when parents need additional context.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.
