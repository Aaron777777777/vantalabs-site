Scheduled Worker Checks

Schedule regular worker checks and review prompt activity, phone checks and relevant incident information from the authorised manager view.

A clearer record for the real working day

Scheduled Worker Checks should solve a recognisable workplace problem rather than simply move an old form onto a phone. The worker-facing part should remain simple: respond to the relevant prompt or follow the agreed workplace check procedure. A documented exception route is just as important as the normal flow.

Managers can use scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information to see which checks are complete and which require a human response. Unusual records still need context before they are corrected or relied upon. Good records depend on a good operating process, especially when the available information is late, incomplete or unexpected. In the context of Scheduled Worker Checks, the organisation should also record who is responsible for resolving any exception.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for scheduled worker checks?

The available record can include scheduled prompts, phone checks and relevant incident information, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can an unusual record be reviewed?

Yes. Authorised managers can examine the available timestamps and context, then follow the organisation’s correction process.

What limitation matters most for scheduled worker checks?

Vanta Workforce supports workplace procedures but is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone’s safety.
