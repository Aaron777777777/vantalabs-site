Making the workplace process easier to follow

A useful remote worker check-in process begins with the real workplace routine, not with the software screen. The app supplies the mobile action; the business supplies the expectation. Here that means a consistent attendance process from a supported mobile device, together with a known support route.

The manager view is intended to make how attendance is recorded across changing sites and field locations easier to review. It presents off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history without pretending that every device event tells the complete story. Rollout should include a short explanation, a test action and a known support route, particularly because of weak connectivity or unclear expectations producing inconsistent records.

What to keep in mind Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for remote worker check-in?

The relevant view can include off-site bookings, field attendance and working-hour history, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?

Mobile attendance still needs a documented fallback for signal, device and account problems.
