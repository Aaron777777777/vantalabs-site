Quick questions

Can I view my work rota across a whole month?

Yes. A monthly calendar can show the shifts and rest days you have entered so the shape of your personal rota is easier to review ahead.

Can I review a whole month?

Yes. Monthly calendar and summary views help show how the saved rota is spread across the month.

Does monthly work rota manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
