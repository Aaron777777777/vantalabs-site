A child location app for quick reassurance

A child location app is most useful when it answers a practical question quickly. Guardian lets trusted adults check the connected child phone’s latest available location without turning the Family Map into something that needs constant attention.

Location sharing for quick reassurance, not constant watching

Battery level and last-update time sit alongside the location so a parent can judge how current the information appears. Home and School alerts can provide routine journey updates before anybody needs to open the map.

The location comes from the connected phone and can be affected by GPS conditions, signal, permissions, battery optimisation and other device restrictions. Guardian therefore provides useful context rather than promising an exact or uninterrupted position.

Family information stays inside the private Guardian family, and adult location sharing remains optional. If there is an immediate safety concern, direct contact and the emergency services are more important than relying on an app update.

Guardian is not an emergency service. Location and notifications can be affected by GPS, connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system restrictions.

Quick questions

What can a parent check in Guardian’s child location view?

Guardian can show the connected child phone’s latest available location together with battery level and last-update time.

Who can see a child device’s location in Guardian?

Access is limited to trusted adults inside the private Guardian family rather than being published through a public profile.

Does the parent have to share their own location?

No. Adult location sharing is optional and can remain off while the child device shares its permitted location information.
