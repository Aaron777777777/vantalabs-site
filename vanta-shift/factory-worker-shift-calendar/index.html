Keep a factory rota clear when shifts keep changing

Factory rotas can combine early starts, late finishes, nights, weekends and rest days. Keeping those dates together makes the next few weeks much easier to read.

Vanta Shift lets you build the repeating pattern, change individual shifts when needed and keep scheduled hours and estimated earnings beside the rota.

Use the real rota as your reference. Vanta Shift is for the individual using the app, not for managing other people.

Quick questions

How can Vanta Shift help factory shift workers?

Factory workers can build their personal shift cycle, adjust individual dates and keep scheduled hours and notes beside the rota they expect to work.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does factory worker shift calendar manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
