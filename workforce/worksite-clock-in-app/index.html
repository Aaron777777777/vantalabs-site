What the organisation should expect from the process

Businesses normally consider worksite clock-in because the current process is slow to check, difficult to consolidate or too dependent on memory. A clear process tells the worker to allow the requested location permission when completing the relevant booking, while also making support available when the normal action cannot be completed.

Authorised managers receive a clearer basis from which to understand whether the available location context matches the expected workplace. The relevant evidence includes booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device. The organisation should review the process after launch and correct any part that creates confusion or unnecessary friction.

GPS and location-aware attendance still needs sensible review. Location can be delayed or inaccurate and should never be treated as infallible proof.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for worksite clock-in?

The available record can include booking time, workplace and the latest location context available from the device, depending on the action completed, device conditions and the organisation’s configuration.

Can device conditions affect the available record?

Yes. Connectivity, permissions, battery settings and operating-system behaviour can affect what reaches the service.

What limitation matters most for worksite clock-in?

Buildings, signal, device permissions and battery restrictions can delay or reduce location accuracy.
