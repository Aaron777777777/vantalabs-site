Quick questions

Can Guardian alert for both leaving and returning Home?

Yes. A configured Home safe zone can provide useful departure and arrival updates from the connected child phone.

Who can see the child device’s Home location information?

Access is limited to trusted adults in the private Guardian family rather than being displayed through public profiles.

Must an adult share their own location to receive child Home alerts?

No. Parent location sharing is optional and can remain off while the family uses child-device Home alerts.
