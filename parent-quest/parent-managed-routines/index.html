Quick questions

Can the parent change a managed routine whenever needed?

Yes. The adult can update tasks, expectations and rewards through the protected parent controls as the family routine changes.

Do parent-managed routines require rewards?

No. Rewards remain optional and under adult control; a routine can be used simply to organise chores and daily responsibilities.

Does a parent-managed routine mean the app supervises the child?

No. Parent Quest protects management and approval controls, but the parent remains responsible for real-world supervision and judgement.
