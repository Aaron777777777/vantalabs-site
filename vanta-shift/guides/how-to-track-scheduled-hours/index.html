How to Track Scheduled Hours

Review the hours created by the shifts currently entered in your calendar and see how your scheduled working time is building across the week or month.

Google Play App Store

Track scheduled hours alongside the rota that creates them

Scheduled hours make more sense when they stay attached to the shifts that produced them. Plan day shifts, night shifts and days off, then review how the hours add up.

Weekly and monthly totals are useful for personal planning rather than as an employer timesheet or payroll record. Update changed shifts so your own totals stay useful.

Use the real rota as your reference. The app helps you organise information but does not create an official rota.

Quick questions

How can I track scheduled working hours?

Record the shifts you expect to work and review the resulting weekly or monthly scheduled-hour totals in your personal calendar.

Will this update my workplace rota?

No. Changes made in Vanta Shift affect only the personal schedule stored in the app.

Does how to track scheduled hours manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
