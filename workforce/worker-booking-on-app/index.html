A practical process for everyday operations

The practical purpose of worker booking on is not simply to digitise a form. It is to produce a clearer record that remains useful after the working day has finished. From the worker’s perspective, success means a repeatable booking-on and booking-off routine. A fair exception route should also exist for genuine mistakes and device problems.

Management value comes from being able to understand when attendance began, when it ended and whether anything is missing. The system organises the start and end points of each recorded attendance period; the manager still decides whether follow-up or correction is appropriate. The app works best inside an explicit operating process. Managers should know how to distinguish a genuine concern from missed book-off actions, late entries or incorrect booking times.

What to keep in mind A booking record supports review but does not automatically explain every workplace circumstance.

Quick questions

What does Vanta Workforce record for worker booking on?

The relevant view can include the start and end points of each recorded attendance period, depending on the worker action, device conditions and organisation setup.

Can workers and managers review the same information?

Access depends on the user’s authorised role. Workers and managers may see different views of the relevant records.

Does the record still need checking?
