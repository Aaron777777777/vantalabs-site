Quick questions

How can I quickly see my upcoming shifts?

Add the shifts you expect to work to your Vanta Shift calendar so upcoming dates, times and rest days can be checked together at a glance.

Are calculated totals official records?

No. Hours and earnings shown by the app are personal planning figures based on the information entered.

Does upcoming shifts app manage other people?

No. Vanta Shift is a personal shift planner for the individual using the app.
