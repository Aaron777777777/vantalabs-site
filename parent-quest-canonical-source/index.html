Parent Quest

Chores, routines and rewards for family life

Give each child a clear list of chores, check what has been done and choose rewards that work for your own family.

Google Play App Store

A calmer way to handle everyday chores

Parent Quest keeps the family chore routine in one place. You choose the jobs, decide which child they belong to and keep track of what still needs doing.

Children can mark a task as finished, but the parent keeps the final say. Once you have checked the job, you can approve it and add reward coins towards something your child is working for.

Built for families, controlled by parents. Parent Quest runs on a parent-owned device. Children do not need separate accounts, and protected actions stay behind the Parent PIN.

Quick questions

What is Parent Quest designed for?

Parent Quest helps parents manage child profiles, chores, tasks, reward coins and custom rewards.

Who approves completed chores?

The parent or guardian reviews progress through the parent-controlled approval flow.

Are reward coins real money?

No. They are parent-managed reward currency inside the family’s own chore and reward system.
