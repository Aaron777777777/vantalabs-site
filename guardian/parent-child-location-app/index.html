A parent and child location app with clear boundaries

Guardian keeps parent and child location features inside a private, adult-managed family. A connected child device shares its permitted information with the trusted adults who have access to that Guardian family.

Quick questions

Is child location sharing separate from parent location sharing in Guardian?

Yes. The connected child device can share its permitted information while adult location sharing remains an optional choice.

Who controls access to parent and child location information?

Guardian uses a private family managed by trusted adults rather than exposing location information through public profiles.

Does Guardian’s SOS feature replace calling emergency services?

No. SOS can help a connected child device reach trusted adults, but Guardian is not an emergency service.
