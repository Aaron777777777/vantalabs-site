A quick way to see what is left

A daily checklist helps children understand progress. They can see what has been marked complete and what is still waiting.

The parent reviews the finished chores before they count towards reward coins, keeping the checklist useful without giving up adult oversight.

A short checklist can also make it easier for children to see what still needs attention without hearing the whole routine repeated again. Parents can keep only the useful daily jobs visible and update the list whenever the household schedule changes.

Marked complete does not mean approved. The parent or guardian checks the work before confirming progress inside the app.

Quick questions

Do all items on a daily chore checklist need to appear every day?

No. Parents decide which chores are genuinely daily and which should only appear when they are needed.

Can siblings use different daily chore checklists?

Yes. Each child can have a separate checklist so the jobs shown match their own routine.

Who approves a completed item on the checklist?

The parent or guardian reviews the child’s completed progress before approval or any related reward progress is confirmed.
