Keep each child’s routine clear

Siblings rarely have exactly the same jobs. Separate child profiles make it easier to give each child a list that suits their age, schedule and responsibilities.

Everything stays together on the parent’s device. You can manage the whole family routine without creating email addresses, passwords or independent logins for children.

Only add what the family needs. A first name or nickname is usually enough to make each child profile easy to recognise.

Quick questions

Can each child have their own profile?

Yes. Each child can have separate chores, progress and rewards.

Do child profiles need separate accounts?

No. They remain part of the parent-managed family setup.

How much personal information is needed?

Parents should only enter what the family needs. A first name or nickname is often enough.
